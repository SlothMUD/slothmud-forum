Since 6/3 the response you get when you cast Putrefy has changed, or more precisely, mostly disappeared. For any mob I’ve tried on VK all I see is “ok” after I cast it. However, the spell is working as seen in the example below:

\# \<166hp 226ma 119mv\>

\# 

\# You hit an evil thug very hard.

\# A ghost pounds an evil thug hard.

\# A ghost bites an evil thug.

\# An evil thug hits you hard.

\# An evil thug misses a ghost with his crush.

\# A ghost pounds an evil thug hard.

\# A ghost bites an evil thug hard.

\# You hit an evil thug very hard.

\# An evil thug is stunned, but will probably regain consciousness again.

\# 

\# \<156hp 226ma 119mv\>

\# 

\# cast 'putrefy'

\# Ok.

\# An evil thug is dead! R.I.P.

Strangely enough, when I was over on BH and tried it on a zombie I did get a descriptive response:

\# cast 'putrefy'

\# Ok.

\# A zombie deterioriates a bit, but doesn't seem to notice.

FYI, typo with “deterioriates,” should be deteriorates. Only noticed because I was using a word processor to type this.
