After receiving several reports regarding Avatar 1 not giving hit and damage, the most recent by Blackmore, I checked out the charts and found the bug.

I was giving 1 hit and 1 damage to anyone with level 0 avatar, that means basically all of you were getting a hit and damage that you were not supposed to. I have fixed this bug which means most of you with 0 avatar levels will be removing a hit and a damage. When you level to avatar 1, you will see a hit increase and level 2 avatar you will see the damage increase.

I have also split the hit and damage up so that they do not come on the same level. I am not sure why I did this because the players get a better feeling when they have something to shoot for on different levels.

Some of you are going to lose a hit and damage and I am sorry.
