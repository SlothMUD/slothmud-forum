Fixed a bug where Gods of War was actually doing less damage than Lion Chorus for the following skills: backstab, grip, wraithtouch, strike,and circle.

This has been fixed and a prime bonus was also added to Gods. These skills all gain +1 damage for Lion(same as before), +2 for gods non-prime, and +3 for gods prime. 

Thank you Josiah for pointing it out and testing!
