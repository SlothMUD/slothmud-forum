To piggyback off of http://www.slothmud.org/forum/viewtopic.php?f=28&t=3845

All caps have been increased according to level 40's gained. Previously, from the post above, there were only 3 cap increases spread across the 9 possible 40 gains. Now, every 40 gained will increase your caps. 

Just for example, it is now possible for a Monk Prime to have 30% damage reduction.

Ultimately, this gives players more equipment options and better characters while giving us Immortals a little more wiggle room when adding equipment.

Change will be in on reboot.
