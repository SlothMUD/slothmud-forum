Greetings.

For some time now it was known that when attempting to insert the glyph to gain rank via one

of the rooms in the Temple of Fiends that it would erroneously take you to the wrong room in

an unconnected area. Well, I'm here to announce this should now be remedied. Those who

are eligible to gain rank at this location may now do so again as normal. If my memory

serves me right (and it may very well not), this is for the rank of protector.

Please let us know if you run into any problems, however. 

Thanks to all of those who were involved with the discovery and fixing of this issue.
