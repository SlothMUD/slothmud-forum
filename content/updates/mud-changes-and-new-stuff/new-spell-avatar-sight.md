"AVATAR SIGHT"

Usage:         cast 'avatar sight'

Cost:          25 mana

Level:         3 Avatar

Duration:      7*levels

Accumulative:  -

Save Versus:   -

Damage Type:   -

Stratum:       Enhancement 

Spellbook:     a mysteriously translucent digest

Prerequisite:  -

See Also:      DETECT 

Angolistui the elf enchantress spent many years searching for treasure

in Moria, she found the incesent casting of detection spells although

infrequent still quite annoying; mainly because they fell at inoportune

moments and only one of them could be recharged.

She spent several weeks with the mages of the tower of Nexialism to

devise a spell that was far more useful.  Although they tried there

hardest they couldn't guarantee to squeeze in darksight like they

wanted, and so this spell annoyingly only appears randomly.
