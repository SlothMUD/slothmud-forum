<div class="post" data-post-id="29173" data-author="*Splork*" data-date="2009-05-15T21:43:00+00:00" data-subject="Bard prime changes">

Two key changes for bard primes to go with the already implemented new slot for instrument....

1\) Bard primes now gain natural charisma with bard levels(similar to monks and damage reduction).

2\) A new level 20 bard prime skill, allows playing an instrument and casting at the same time. Help file below.

help adaptation

ADAPTATION TO CIRCUMSTANCES

See Also: cast play sing

Level: Bard prime 20

Manual: a guide to adaptation of circumstances

Duration: Immediate

After years of practice, Jacinto has mastered the art of casting spells while

playing his instrument non-stop. While he was tempted to keep this secret

to himself, he has decided to share it with other bard pupils. Adaptation to

circumstances has always been the key to survival, inside this book, one will

learn an ancient secret which allows a player to adapt their instrumental

playstyle and incorporate their ability to cast at the very same time.

Enjoy,

Splork

</div>

<div class="post" data-post-id="29184" data-author="*Splork*" data-date="2009-05-16T13:20:47+00:00" data-subject="Bard mana regen">

1\) Bard prime and secondary have had their mana regen increased. 

We will not be making anymore changes to bards until we see where we are with this week's changes. We should be pretty close to where we want to be.

The instrument change, mr change, dual casting change, and increased charisma should go a very long way in giving these players more solo ability, less regen time, and overall more fun gameplay, especially in groups where they no longer have to just sit there and sing.

Enjoy,

Splork

If anyone finds any bugs in the dual casting, please let me know! I would greatly appreciate it.
