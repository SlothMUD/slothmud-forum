After speaking with several druids and doing a comparison of the damage done in form to what they can do at their current levels, Ive come to the conclusion that forms needed a small adjustment.

In order to make the progress more linear between forms, I had to lower Naga's ac by .2. Overall, this allowed for a much better progression. 

Ac, damage, hit points, and number of attacks have all been increased in a uniform manner.

Please remember, forms were grossly overpowered in S3 and we will never allow that to happen again. Enhancements will occur in small doses until we fell that shapeshifting is a viable option.( which I feel it is:)

Enjoy,

Splork

The changes are from Naga - Dragon.
