Master Hawthorn is now offering daily bounties on mobs and weekly/quarterly bounties on forges. Simply go to him in BH town and type 'list'. Some comments:

 Daily subdue bounties reset every day at midnight server time.

 The weekly forge bounty resets every week at midnight as Saturday rolls to Sunday.

 The quarterly forge bounty resets every quarter at midnight as the calendar rolls to Jan 1, April 1, July 1, and October 1.

 The daily mob bounties and the weekly forge bounty each have a difficulty rating from 1 to 10 (1 being easiest, 10 being hardest). These difficulty ratings cannot be compared across bounties. In other words, a difficulty rating of 10 in the 1-to-5-mil-xp mob bounty is not the same difficulty as a difficulty rating of 10 in the 5-to-10-mil xp mob bounty.

 The quarterly forge bounty has a difficulty rating of either 15 or 20 (15 means moderately difficult, 20 means extremely difficult). 

 Rewards are scaled according to difficulty of the bounty. 

 Bounties should be different for different players, so we shouldn't be tripping over each other. I made sure to structure the code so that no one player has access to higher difficulty (and thus higher reward) bounties. For example, one player's weekly bounty may only be a difficulty of 2, but another player's weekly bounty may be a difficulty of 10--over time this will average out. 

As always, please let me know if you run into anything busted with this new feature. Enjoy!
