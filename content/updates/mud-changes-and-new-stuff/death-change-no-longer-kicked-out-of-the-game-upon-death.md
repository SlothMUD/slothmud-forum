I've tweaked the corpse code slightly. For those of you who are going to corpse and either quit out or time out, you will get a few messages, be prompted to press return, and then u are back at the SlothMUD log in screen and all you will have to do is press 1 to reenter the game.

<-12hp 485ma 148mv>

You have died and been brought back to the SlothMUD log in screen.

Press 1 to reenter the game and then go about quickly recovering your corpse.

Feel free to ask for help on the newbie channel, they will often help you retrieve it!

****  Press enter to continue and log back in  ****

Welcome to the Sloth DikuMUD at UKANS

0) Exit from DikuMud.

1) Enter the game.

2) Enter description.

3) Read the background story

4) Change password.

5) Delete this character. [Level 9 or less only]

Only players of low level will see the third msg above regarding help recovering their corpses.
