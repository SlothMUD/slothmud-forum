Hi all ...

1\) You will get a message about number of "pages" left when you start reading a book (pages meaning ticks) :

 \<251hp 477ma 258mv\>

 You begin reading a bejeweled book of conflagration ... it has 7 pages.

 \<251hp 477ma 258mv\>

2\) You will get a message about number of "pages" left every time you turn page (pages meaning ticks) :

 \<251hp 477ma 258mv\>

 You turn to the next page in a bejeweled book of conflagration ... there are 6 pages left.

 \<254hp 477ma 258mv\>

3\) Fixed that you wont stop studying when you are ALREADY studying and try to read spellbook.

4\) You will get a message when you finish reading an spellbook in a guild and there are other spellbooks in floor (everyday newbie question) :

 \<260hp 477ma 244mv\>

 You finish reading a worn leather bound spellbook, and close the cover.

 You see other spellbooks on the floor.

 You may want to type read 2.spellbook, read 3.spellbook, etc.

 \<262hp 477ma 250mv\>

5\) You will get a message about the spell when you do examine the book, depending if you know the spell (whatever 1% or 95%) or not :

 \<266hp 477ma 258mv\>

 You see nothing special..

 You see the words 'armor' written on the cover.

 \<266hp 477ma 258mv\>

 or

 \<265hp 477ma 258mv\>

 You see nothing special....It emits a faint humming sound!

 You see some strange words written on the cover.

 \<265hp 477ma 258mv\>
