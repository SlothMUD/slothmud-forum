Greetings, mortals.

From the next crash or reboot, the game will keep track of what happens with

equipment, so our new equipment page will show the right information. It is

going to work this way ...

There are three different parts in the equipment page for every item : the item

name, the item stats and who pops it.

1\) The web page has a full database with all our items and stats. It do not

 mean that everything will be showed right away.

2\) The game will generate a list with every item that everyone has when they

 do rent. This list will be feed also with anything that is identified and

 and with anything that pops. Anything in this list will be show in the

 equipment page. We are speaking here about the plain item name, nothing

 else.

3\) The game will generate a list with every item that is identified : could be

 via scroll/spell or via shop. Anything in this list will show stats in

 the equipment page.

4\) The game will generate a list with every item that is poped. With an item

 poped we mean that the game generate it : its not needed that a player takes

 it or even that anyone notices its there. Anything in this list will show

 the mob poping it in the equipment page. Note that if an item is poped for

 more than one mob, all of them will be showed. Note also that random pops

 will never make it into this list.

And i think it is all. During a few weeks/months, there will be odd stuff,

like items with stats and withouth saying who pop it ... items saying who pop

it and withouth saying stats. Even items with only the name.

As you all are seeing, its a job of everyone to have a decent equipment list.

Mud just will assure that anything showed is right, but its a job of the players

to get those lists filled, poping stuff even if they do not need them,

identifying items of what they already know stats, etc. Its the most fair way

we have found to automatically generate info from the mud side withouth giving

everything out all the sudden.
