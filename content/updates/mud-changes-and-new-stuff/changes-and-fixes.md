3\) Kjartan just finished coding a couple group commands that were suggested by Alberich or Plato, we cant remember which. 

GSET" 

See also: GROUP UNGROUP 

This command has two forms. It can only be used by the leader of a group. 

The first form is "gset disband". This ends the group, ungrouping everybody 

and stopping everybody from following the leader. 

The second form is "gset transfer \<target\>". This transfers the group to 

the specified person, who must be a member of the group. That person 

becomes the new group leader.
