Flawed gem no longer has a drachma resale value. 

Autoquest 'vial' retired.

Snare drum autoquest at enslaved zodiac mage retired. 

Snare drum forge moved to Ritornello, Unseen Master of Music.

Several new autoquests available at the new Aisholm quester, Forsythe, near wide gate.

The bottom parts of cleric tower have changed. The miners are wounded aggressive and assist other mobs. The damage is slightly increased. The gold and xp is much increased. The gold on enslaved mage is decreased. The bone key gets eaten now, but the door is pick level 2 from the other side, so player can get out in case of reset. The mage cell is nomagic and nogate. One of the new autos from quester involves mage.

Watch for changes around autoquest mobs and surrounding vicinities.
