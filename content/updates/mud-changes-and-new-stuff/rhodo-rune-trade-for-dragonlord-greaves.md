Announcing a new trade: Greaves of the Dragonlords, .5 AC, *shapeshift, 8x40 / supreme druid restricted

          5 million coins

Trader: Vicor, the blue dragon at the top of the Spire of Good and Evil

Rationale:

After a close examination of max_stats as they could possibly relate to shifted players, it was determined that the existing status quo will not change, i.e. max stat shift gear will not affect the stats of shifted players.

Out of a sense of fairness tho, we have created the above noted item so that druids can at least benefit from forging a Rhodochrosite Rune.
