Greeting mortals.

- We have changed the items you get when using the spell succor.  Changed the short description, added new keywords and also added them a timer (one mud day for scrolls, two and a half mud days for staves).

Succor was never supposed to replace the buying of scrolls and staves, was more like an emergency spell in case you ran out.  We have (intentionally) leave it alone for a long time, but the ridiculous abuse that people has been doing lately, that resulted in T&D having over a thousand scrolls has forced us to fix it.

Change is already in-game.

Best regards.
