"CIRCLE OF HEALING"

Usage: cast 'circle of healing'

Cost: 65

Level: Cleric 40

Duration: -

Accumulative: -

Save Versus: -

Damage Type: -

Stratum: healing

Spellbook: ancient tome of mass heal

Prerequisite: -

See Also: HEAL RESTORATION GREATER HEAL

This spell can heal everybody in a group, as long as they are

in the same room as the caster. The healing power increases

with the total levels of the character in question. In essence, the

spell's power grows as the player grows.
