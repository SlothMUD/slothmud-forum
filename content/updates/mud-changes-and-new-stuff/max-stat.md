Here is a small chart that shows when each of your stats can be taken higher.

Remember, you need to use max\_stat eq to do this.

level Class Stat ordering

1x40 19, 18, 18, 18, 18

2x40 20, 19, 18, 18, 18

3x40 21, 20, 19, 18, 18

4x40 22, 21, 20, 19, 18

5x40 23, 22, 21, 20, 19

6x40 24, 23, 22, 21, 20

7x40 24, 23, 22, 21, 20

8x40 25, 24, 23, 22, 21

Each Max Stat gets bonuses:

Max\_Con Extra hit points, extra hit point regen

Max Dex -.5 AC per stat

Max Str Extra damage and extra hitroll

Max Wis Extra Mana Regen and divine focus

Max Int Extra Mana, extra mems, and arcane focus

Divine Focus is when a cleric or druid spell would normally lose concentration, that

spell gets rolled a second time using your max\_stat bonus and hopefully succeeds.

Arcane Focus is when a necro or mage spell would normally lose concentration, that

spell gets rolled a second time using your max\_stat bonus and hopefully succeeds.

The bonus for arcane and divine focus improves with each max\_stat gained.
