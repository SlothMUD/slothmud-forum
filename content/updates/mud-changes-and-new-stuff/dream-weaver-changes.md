After today your letters of introduction will no longer work (once you rent or otherwise d/c).  

The Dreamweaver has decided to keep a ledger with members contained inside.  To update your membership he requests that you give him your old letter so that he can update his ledger.  If you are new, and have not yet recieved a letter, you can still pay 30 drachma to the Dreamweaver to obtain a valid membership.  See the ledger in the Dreamweaver's shop for more information.

With this new method of keeping track of members, the Dreamweaver has agreed to once again sell octagonal tokens inside the Square.
