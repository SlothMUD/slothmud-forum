<div class="post" data-post-id="35610" data-author="*juggleblood*" data-date="2015-01-02T18:21:01+00:00" data-subject="Summary of Coliseum 3.0">

*First off, instead of tearing out the old mobs and putting in new, I built a second coliseum in AH, which is not open yet.

*The BH coliseum scores are reset and you can earn drachma and honor. Honor reward is now based off of personal best rather than high scorer. There is no benefit to getting in sooner than everyone else to get a high score. There is no high scorer drachma or honor. So don't be in a hurry to get in there. Focus on bug testing for now if you play in it.

*You will be dispelled on entrance to the coliseum. So will your pets. So no need spelling up or having someone else spell you up. You will not receive a restore. So go in full.

*You will receive mana restores at the 2, 4, and 6 minute marks (roughly). The timer is not very accurate, should be within 10 seconds either way though. You will not receive hit points. 

*There are no berries.

*Mobs are being slimmed down in the higher levels. It is not complete. Need to get some more score data and tweak them accordingly.

*Mobs in BH coliseum are basically the same, some only slimmer. As before, sword is for demons, spear for dragons, breath for death, and salt for snail. 

It's a work in progress but it is open for testing. I ask you to honor the spirit of the coliseum rule about outside aid and avoid doing the following:

Using imbued spells.

Using charmed pets (charm person).

Using devaks.

Having a friend level a 40 to get a world restore during fight.

Also familiar may be exploitable in that it could end up counting as a point, I'll have to check and add protection if so. Don't do it... I'll test it later.

I will slowly implement protections against these practices but in the meantime I'll just have to resort to investigating suspicious scores.

I encourage you to test levels Newbie-4 and level 7. You may want to hold off on 5, 6, 8, and 9. I have minor mob modification to do. 

You can email me bugs or suggestions to juggleblood@slothmud.org or u can use ingame mudmail system or u can use the bugs command.

</div>

Levels 5-9 are re-balanced and much better imo, i.e. you should see your scores improve.

Don't forget to take advantage of the holy sword of defenders versus demons.

Some other non-stat based changes:

*Tanra paralysis proc reduced from 50 seconds to 10 seconds.

*Greek god mobs have minor changes to names, i.e. Athena became avatar of Athena. In deference to the proper mobs on Mount Olympus.

*Aeris God of War, is now avatar of Aeris, God of Battle, to avoid conflict with player name War in chaos pit.

*Demon mobs have minor short name changes to indicate they are in fact demons, i.e. to use sword of defenders.

Feel free to send me suggestions and bug reports via ingame ideas and bugs commands as well as mudmail, forum posts, or juggleblood@slothmud.org. *edit - email corrected* However pls inform me if you are emailing me for the first time so I can check my junk mail and add you as a contact.

I'm interested in hearing how all the various procs play out and interested in suggestions regarding mob alignment in the pits and anything else you have to say.

There are still ways to cheat. Please don't do it. I'm working on fixes. In the meantime I'm just occasionally pulling logs on high scorers.
