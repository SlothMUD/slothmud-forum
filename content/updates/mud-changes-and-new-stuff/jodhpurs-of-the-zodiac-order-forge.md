There is a new forge available, designed by Pixie, called Jodhpurs of the Zodiac Order. 

There are 2 mobs involved, Cheiron King of the Centaurs, and Cassiopeia the Stargazing Beauty on the High Plain on Thordfalan.

The cps are located globally and will be a challenge to find. Cheiron gives the names of the components, so you can use wealth and glory to help locate them. I encourage someone to start a separate thread for players to share information on this forge.

Credit to Pixie. Enjoy.
