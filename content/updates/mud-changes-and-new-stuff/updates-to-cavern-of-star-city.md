Ever get stuck in this area when you were young, and couldn't find your way out?

Ever forget your recall and have to get someone to bring you one?

well, fret no more! You can now 'hunt' Dophrael, and once you reach him, you can

transfer out of the area back to South Gate in Newbatia by issuing one of the three

following commands: 

1. click heels

2. snap fingers

3. recall

This means you don't 'say' them, but you simply type them as you would any other 

command in the game.

Enjoy.
