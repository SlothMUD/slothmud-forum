At the request of one of our players, whom uses a screen reader, the following settings option has been added, comms\_only. 

Toggle this on, and all room descriptions, objects, characters, and messages regarding leaving and arriving have been gagged.

This will help those players have conversations without their screen readers getting too much output to follow correctly.

Enjoy,

Splork
