I have not been keeping the website up to date with ingame changes so here are a few since our last posting. I've been keeping ingame NEWS up to date for almost two years, simply type NEWS ingame for a thorough listing. Also, we will probably tie an option into live info for news which can be clicked and read the ingame news boards soon...

SEPTEMBER 3

1\) Mxp on/off can now be used to toggle mxp

2\) Numformat in settings now shows correctly

3\) MXP options now remember their settings(turning MXP off

 will no longer remove all your mxp settings. When you

 toggle mxp back on, your previous settings will reset

 themselves).

4\) Finger always works on your own character

5\) Restiveness down msg added

6\) Players will now see what bard songs are affecting them in score

7\) Quite a few other fixes(spellbooks, help files, etc)

<img src="/images/smilies/icon_cool.svg" alt="8)" title="8)" width="15" height="15" class="smiley nozoom"> Kill overides kiss when using shorthand cmd, ie ki k kil etc(please be careful)

AUGUST 29

1\) Locker bug preventing some eq from being deposited is fixed.

2\) The big druid/KVP config bug has been fixed. This solves several shifted

 problems we have had the last few months.

3\) Deathblow has been slightly reworked-msgs/items dependant upon species.

4\) Several new help files have been added or reworked to work with MXP-help mxp

5\) Stun and focused stun have a higher rate of success.

AUGUST 8

1\) Wear all, wear weapon will now remove flurry affect if a weapon is weielded

2\) Keys used to enter areas can no longer be used for fleeing

3\) Swordwraiths and Phantoms have been slightly lowered(wraithform removed)

4\) The initital stages of MSP are ingame. Help msp for more information

5\) Bug affecting mobs with high hitrolls and wielding weapons doing little

 to no damage has been fixed

6\) Newbie restricted eq can no longer be worn by avatars

7\) MXP has been added to objects(for imms)

<img src="/images/smilies/icon_cool.svg" alt="8)" title="8)" width="15" height="15" class="smiley nozoom"> Monk skills have been added to mob abilities-enjoy this one(check Lan out:-)

9\) Mounts will now return to their shops if they are not being ridden after

 about 5-10 minutes. This is to make sure our shops always have mounts to sell.

10\) Riposte messages have been fixed

11\) Delesor's pass has been fixed

12\) Dark mace has been made personal

13\) Shifted druids no longer gain 2 hits from haste

14\) Lurk and MSP added to settings cmd

15\) Bug allowing teachers to teach students whom are asleep is fixed.

JULY 24

1\) Warcry has been added to finger.

2\) The hitrate for Stomp has been increased.

JULY 18

1\) I have recently worked out a method for animating partial skeletons

as an exercise to be practiced by apprentice necromancers who are not yet

ready to attempt an entire reanimation. See "help animate dead" for details.

The spell can now be cast by necromancers as low as the sixth rank.

\----Azzenon

2\) A bug affecting the hitrate of Deathtouch has been squashed.

\- Azzenon
