I've just finished and committed the majority of the code needed for the tailoring of equipment and weapons. A basic summary of the idea is this: 

1\) You gather five of the same gemstones. 

2\) You sing the song "call of the cuillen" \<rune stone name\>

3\) If all goes well, the sidhe leave you with the runestone

4\) You may then socket the rune stone into the armor or weapon that it is meant for. Atm, we are only allowing certain rune stones to be placed into certain wear locations. 

This idea has been coded to fit our game but the basic system is based off of Diablo 2 rune and sockets. I have never played the game myself but the idea was given to me by a couple players who had.

I believe this will add a ton of fun to our game while allowing our players the ability to tailor their eq to their play style. The choices for each locations are tough ones, designed to really make you think about how you want to play your character.
