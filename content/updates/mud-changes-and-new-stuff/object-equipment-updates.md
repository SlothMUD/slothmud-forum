The book for the 22nd level warrior skill "Stomp" has been moved from Marta

the librarian in Newbatia to off-Valkyre sites. This was done beecause it didn't

make sense to have a 22nd level skill's book on a continent where only 20th level

and below players could reach. This book was moved to three mobs around the

world. These mobs are listed below, and the EQ list should update accordingly

whenever it gets updated.

Royal Guard, Kingdom of Luilekkerland, Thule

Uruk-Hai Warrior, Moors of Gr'Valdh, Island

Young Squire, Castle of the Family DeGrance, Thordfalan

Enjoy.

-Neptune

Greetings

The Fangs of the Fire Serpent clasp has been changed to be removable. This was done for a couple reasons:

1. Runes could not be removed via ingame normal procedures because of the inability to remove the clasp

    to perform such actions. 

2. Being the lesser of the two merits between the boots of serpentinous demise and fangs clasp combination

    when compared to the demonskin boots forge, it was reasonable to allow the removal of the clasp for other

    purposes. 

No other changes to the clasp were made. No changes to any other items were made at this time.

Hope that helps, guys.

-Neptune
