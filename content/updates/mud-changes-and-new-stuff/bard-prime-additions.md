1\) dance with the dead

"DANCE WITH THE DEAD"

See Also: cast play sing death

Level: Bard prime 30

Manual: A guide of preparation

Duration: innate

This is an innate skill. Once this skill is learned, the bard prime has the

keen ability to escape a creature's killing blow by simply calling forth

the song of their predecessor's called Dance with the Dead. This song

bursts through the heavens and rains down upon the lonely bard causing

him to dance away from the mortal blow.

Enjoy,

Splork
