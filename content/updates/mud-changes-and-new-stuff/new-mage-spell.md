New mage spell called Sandstorm..

"SANDSTORM"

See also:     -

Usage:        cast 'sandstorm'

Accumulative: No

Duration:     2 hours

Level:        mage 35

Save:         -

Spellbook:

Stratum:      Shaping

Min Mana:     40

The mage calls forth strong gusts of wind which ultimately create twisting

sandstorms throughout the land. The combination of wind, dust, and sand

causes much dismay to creatures caught inside. These beasts often find

themselves struggling in battle, as their vision becomes grossly

distorted.

Enjoy,
