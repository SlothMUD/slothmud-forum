The other day, I was visiting the town of Newbatia on the continent of

Valkyre. It was a friendly visit, and so I decided to drop in at the home

of Sir Virdo, the Mayor of Newbatia. I was quite pleased as what I had 

learnt while the two of us swapped stories and drank a few pints that

evening. It appears as if the residents of Newbatia have begun to get

the itch to stake their claims elsewhere. Some say that it might be

getting a bit crowded with all the newcomers. I can't be sure, but if

that's the case, then where are they going? Well, I asked Sir Virdo

that very question, and you know what he told me? He said that a few

people loaded up their carts and went to stake their claim in the wooded

areas north of Newbatia. It seems as if they intend to settle down and

start farming there. The first of these people was a guy by the name of 

Cletus and his wife Winnifred, the mayor said. I think I'll go have a talk

with that couple tomorrow maybe, and see how they are doing. And if 

you find yourself up that way, stop by and welcome them to the area.

That'd be a swell gesture, don't you think?

\-Neptune
