Greetings

I am happy to announce that your immortal staff will be undergoing a review of existing areas

in the game that have existed for a while, and seem to have been left behind in our ever-moving

forward and progression of the game. As time passes, somethings get left behind and stagnate.

We have heard your criticisms and offered some of our own over a wide swath of zones that

are currently in the game. As such, we have elected to begin a new campaign of sorts for the

upcoming year or so as time allows.

From this time forward, we will be updating and bringing several areas and zones forward while

updating to them to the current game. In some cases, these changes will be minor. In some cases,

these changes may be a little more than minor. This will include new mobs, new EQ, new rooms,

and even new features that will further expand our experiences as players adventuring within these

zones. 

In the future, you can look to this thread to keep you updated as to which zones have been updated.

Furthermore, I'd like to re-inroduce our first zone of this campaign: The River of Blood. Please take

the time to check it out and let us know of anything that's not quite right or doesn't work right. But

we hope this helps you guys out and gives you a few more options for gameplay as well as providing

new and interesting flavors and challenges in the times ahead.

Enjoy, and as always, thanks for playing!

-Neptune

Greetings

I have taken the time to review Mount An' Mystica, and have addressed some of the concerns regarding

this area. I have addressed the concerns folks had about the Carnivorous Apes. I have heard and

addressed the concerns players have had with Mystica's difficulty and overall ornery nature, making it

difficult to simply explore in there much less fight anything.

Starting now, the entire area is now open to temp items. Beforehand, no temp items were allowed.

As a result, some of the mobs have been converted to chop mobs providing you guys with a few

more optionsfor lyme chop mobs. (Sorry no more new epic chop mobs :P). A new piece of EQ was

added to Mystica as well as an AQ or two. For the most p0art, the mobs were revamped with the

exception of one or two. This doesn't mean that they are necessarily nerfed or boosted. You will

just have to figure that out for yourselves.

Anyways, enjoy. And as always, if you find anything wonky, hit me up ingame.
