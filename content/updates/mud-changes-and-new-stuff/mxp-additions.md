1) new element RoomExits(will set your mxp variables to all the open exits in the room

2) Shop items are now hyperlinked(you can buy with the click of the mouse-or right click and query or buy depending on level)

3) Exits in the exits cmd are now hyperlinked.

4) Exits on look now have a menu if right clicked. This will cause a menu consisting of look dir, scan dir, or move in direction. Or you can still simply click to move.

5) Grouptells have been opened up to safe mxp tags. These tags consist of underline, color, font, bold, strikeout, high, and italic).

Shop addition was put ingame by Isabo couple days ago, The others will be in on reboot tomorrow morning since I just commited them.
