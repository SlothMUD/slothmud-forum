ISATAR

See also: Newbie

An Isatar is a player who is dedicated to helping teach newbies the ways

of SlothMUD. All those with the rank of Isatar are hand picked by the

creators, and have a great deal of mudding experience on SlothMUD. 

Isatars are lower level characters played by players who have higher level

characters and a vast knowledge of the SlothMUD world and game play style. 

All those with the title of Isatar will have it shown prior to their name

as a type of Pre-Title. They will always be level 20 prime or under, as 

this will allow them to go anywhere a newbie may travel.

A sample of someone on the who list that is an isatar would look like:

[20w 20m 20c 12t] ISATAR Tisha the newbie helper.

Also, the Isatar pre-title will be set in the color red to help stand out

even more to those players that choose to use our color system. To turn

the SlothMUD color system on, just type color and it will be turned on.

If you seek the help of someone who is an Isatar, please feel free to ask

them for help either by tell playername Can you help me, or use the newbie

channel.

If you are someone who wishes to become an Isatar and help newbies learn the

ways of sloth, please email Blaster or Splork at blaster@slothmud.org and.

splork @slothmud.org. They will always be willing to talk to you and give

you more details about the Isatar program. Please understand, we are

looking for those players who know the mud very well and are able to teach 

the true new players the ways of our game. Being an Isatar has its own 

special rewards.
