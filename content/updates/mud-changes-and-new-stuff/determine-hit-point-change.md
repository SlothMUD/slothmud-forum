Due to having max stats to 25 now, we started this version of Sloth with a few points of Constitution which had the same hitpoint bonus as the stat before it. 

Constitution points of 11-13 were the same bonus, 14-15 were the same, and 16-17 gave the same bonus.

Now, every point of Con gives you a bonus, starting at 12. Each point raises by .5 and maxes out at a bonus of 7.5 at 25 Con.

Enjoy,

Splork

\*\* Keep in mind, not all classos get bonuses for each stat of constitution. If you are one of those classos, you will not see a change \*\*
