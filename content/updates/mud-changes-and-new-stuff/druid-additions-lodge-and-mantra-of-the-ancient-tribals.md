Devaks are lodged deep into the earth's crust to create a magical tie

between one of the seven strata. Once an area has been affected by this

item, all magic of this particular stratum cast within the entire area

is affected one way or the other. The length of affects felt from the

devak are dependant upon the character's total levels. As the player

grows, so does the strength of his devak.

Please refer to help lodge, help devak, and help mantra for more information regarding these changes.

Enjoy,

Splork

<div class="post" data-post-id="29717" data-author="*Splork*" data-date="2009-07-22T23:46:32+00:00" data-subject="natures upheaval">

help natures upheaval

"NATURES UPHEAVAL"

Usage:         cast 'natures upheavel'

Cost:          65 mana

Level:         Druid 40

Duration:      -

Accumulative:  -

Save Versus:   -

Damage Type:   -

Stratum:       Summoning

Spellbook:     an earth toned spellbook

Prerequisite:  -

See Also:      DEVAK MANTRA LODGE PENDULUM

See Also:      STRATA SIDHE CHARMS PERISTRATA

This spell causes the earth's crust to shake uncontrollably until it spits the

the Devak right out of the ground. This spell can only be cast upon a totem that

has been placed there by the caster. Attempting to remove another person's devak

can be extremely dangerous and should be done at one's own risk.

Let it be known, devaks are controlled by the stratum of which they are named for.

Due to the inconsistencies of such magic, devaks are known to crumble and

disappear after a certain duration. Smart magicians have been known to lodge

and cast this spell several times a day to prevent the loss of their ever-so-valuable

devak.

Enjoy,

Splork
