A charm is now given to the player at the same time the Necromancer prime preforms their ritual and creates their JuJu Zombie. This charm will crumble upon the JuJu's death. However, if the mud happens to crash or the player needs to rent, the zombie is sucked inside the charm and frozen for later use. When the player re-enters the game, they may summon juju or summon zombie with the charm in their inventory and the creature will magically appear before them ready to serve their master's every wish.
