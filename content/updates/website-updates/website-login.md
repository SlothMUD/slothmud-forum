Players can now log into the website here at [http://www.slothmud.org](http://www.slothmud.org) using their in-game character name and the password they have set for this login using the newly created wwwpass command. 

Please remember, the website has one login, and the forum has a totally separate login( Confusing, yes!). The websites login is your character name and the password you set inside of Sloth. Your forum login is whatever name you created and the password you gave to that a long time ago.

***** Upon the initial setting of a password using wwwpass command, the player needs to log out of sloth and back into sloth for the password to take affect on the website *****

Currently, your login is tied to such things are your playerstats page and any live auctions you may have:

http://www.slothmud.org/wp/live-info/live-auctions

<LINK_TEXT text="http://www.slothmud.org/wp/live-info/pl ... cs?pc=Burl">http://www.slothmud.org/wp/live-info/player-statistics?pc=Burl</LINK_TEXT>

Custom Menus added to your login block => click 'Add to Menu | Remove from Menu' at the bottom of most              pages to add them to a custom menu

However, we have some great ideas and plans to extend this ability in the future to include things such as avatars, finished quests, character description, etc, etc.

We will continue to post additions as they are added,

Splork & Breeze
