How about making an animal clans system for druids? I suggest making a 3x40 body item (like Shroud of Ancient Druid 0 AC +1 hpreg +1mareg +mvregen) AND a bunch of different forged shiftable clasps which can be only attached to this armor, cannot be detached and give some bonuses to a corresponding form. Bonuses can be the same for all forms (like +1% hp for each 2 levels above form level - so 5x40 will receive 20% bonus to dragon, 39% bonus to ferret and no bonus for piasa) or can differ from form to form (ferret don't need many hp, hehe, but mv regen would help). We can even unite some forms in one clan - Snake Clan can include Asp, Cobra and possibly Naga, same with spiders, dragons, birds, etc.

Also, since all 3x40 items have bonuses that could be used by all classes, those clasps can give bonuses for human form as well - like spellbonus for dragon clan, +stab dam for snakes, perma Stone/Iron Skin for trees :) and so on.
