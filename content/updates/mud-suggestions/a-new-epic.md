This is the natural flow and ebb of mud. The summer always dries up and activity always picks up in the fall and peaks around new year.

I took Popeye up on his suggestion of loading epics with drachma because our numbers were weak that week. I watch that kind of thing. It isn't so much the drachma that draws people, but the knowledge that's there's going to be a decent group plus the personal invite on the events email list.

So anytime someone wants to put out an invite for people to join a group you plan to lead, let an imm know and they'll get it out to the events list for you.

I won't be nerfing any of the epics because the players grow into them. The power of the players grows exponentially in time. You end up with situations where two avatars are arguing over which one gets to solo a mob that's supposed to take a large group. 

Activity is picking up, so who knows what will come.
