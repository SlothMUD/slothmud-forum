<div class="post" data-post-id="14477" data-author="Atwell" data-date="2006-10-03T12:14:38+00:00" data-subject="">

You can identify elite equipment (equipment you purchase with drachma) by looking for Found on Mobile of sir faur, high priest, adante, or trista. Quest equipment (equipment given out in an immortal-run quest) usually has a Found on Mobile of Quest, Quest Low, Quest Medium, or Quest High. Items one receives upon completing an autoquest somtimes have a Found of Mobile containing "autoquest", but other times they have the AutoQuest flag, and Found on Mobile is the mob offering the auto.
