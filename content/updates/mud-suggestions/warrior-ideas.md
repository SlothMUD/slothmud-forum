I've been kicking around ideas for warriors, and here are a couple that may or may not be worthy of the mud.  These won't exactly aid in battle... but they may help out all the same.

1.  Examine skill:  Much like the condition of a mob you would see if you typed look, but only provides you the condition of a mob...   2 differences, first, it would have more levels.. say 15 or 20, so you'd have a much better idea of how damaged a mob is.  Second, you wouldn't see the description and eq, ect.  Could be useful in combat without giving a direct advantage.

2.  Spoil skill:  Allows you to carve up a corpse looking for hidden valuables (such as slicing open a dragon's stomach).  Basically a second chance at popping a cp. I'm thinking this should be limited to cp type items, in the intrest of not making it too powerful... but whatever works is fine.  Give warriors a slight advantage in completing forges.  Then again, maybe this skill is better suited to thievse?  Dunno, heh, give it to both :)
