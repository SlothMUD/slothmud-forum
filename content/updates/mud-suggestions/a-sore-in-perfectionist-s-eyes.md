"RULE 1" "RULE ONE" MULTIPLAYING

1)  No Multiplaying.

====================

Muliplaying is the act of having more than one character simultaneously

visible within the game of Sloth, whether the link is active or not.

There are NO circumstances where one may play two characters at the same

time.  This includes playing two characters from separate machines or

separate windows on the same machine at the same time.

You MUST ALWAYS rent one character before logging on another.  Therefore(COMMA)

DO NOT just cut your link before logging on another character.

Asking for help while one of your other characters is dead IS NOT permitted.

You MAY NOT pass equipment between two characters owned by you which have

both been logged on by you, not even in an Inn. (IN THE INN or AT THE INN?)

You MAY NOT mortally wound a mob(COMMA?) then log on another character of yours to

kill it and gain experience.  Even if your other character has logged off.

You MAY use one character to collect the corpse of another of your

characters, as long as only one character is on at any time.

Multiplaying WILL be punished.

"RULE 2" "RULE TWO" "PLAYER LOOTING" PLOOTING

a player has corpsed and deliberately not returning it to the rightful

owner.

The only cases were player looting is allowed is after an outlawed player

is killed.  The player would have had their name on the outlaw board.

Non sanctioned player looting is subject to harsh penalties.

h rule 3

"RULE 3" "RULE THREE" "PLAYER KILLING" "PKILLING"

3)  No Player Killing.

======================

Player killing is not permitted without a good reason (and this reason

will have to (BE?) explained to the immortal that deals with this problem).

If you are going to kill someone for stealing from you, make quite certain

that you have the right culprit - or you could get in trouble yourself.

"RULE 4" "RULE FOUR" ROBOT BOTTING

4)  No Robotting.

Robotting is the act of setting up your MUD client to run the character on

its own.  Playing a 'robot' character is forbidden, and will be punished.

You ARE NOT allowed to program your MUD client to allow your buddy to

have full control over your character.

Allowing the leader to set your targets,(NO COMMA?) and "all up" triggers are fine.
