We have these boards with note dispensers, why not turn them into something useful ?

Players post their ideas/suggestions, same as you'd post on a board.

After reading this message any other player can either sign for it or against it and total votes are tallied, posts with best yay to nay ratio get sent to immortals for review and posts with crappy player reviews get deleted.

This would introduce a democratic aspect to our gamechanging additions and reduce the ideas load on our immortals who wouldn't have to read every crappy idea submitted to them.

<div class="post" data-post-id="36340" data-author="Teron" data-date="2015-12-22T07:11:22+00:00">

You know, I've had the same idea recently: we need to collect input from the players ingame and see the status of our ideas/suggestions.

The forum is nice, but it is not a task board for immortals and we don't see any decision-makin or progress until they've done it.

My suggestion would be:

- we need a board, where players can see all the suggestions

- for each suggestion/idea, we need to see development progress: is it seen, denied, requires more info, in discussion, in development, in testing, does it need testing w/ mortals, is it live on the server?

- ideally, an idea thread would contain comments from mortals/immortals

Basically, Trello for ideas to improve the MUD that players can use from inside the MUD. Though for starters, I'd just welcome this on the website or as a Trello/other task board: seeing progress and where player feedback is needed is precious, because it increases communication between players and the immortals.
