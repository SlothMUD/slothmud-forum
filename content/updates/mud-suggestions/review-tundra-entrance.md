<div class="post" data-post-id="34956" data-author="Hung" data-date="2014-04-04T10:16:54+00:00" data-subject="Review Tundra Entrance">

The Tundra Area Entrance is abruptly dangerous. It starts with silver worgs, which are easy 100-200k. Then suddenly there are winter wolves and large bucks, which are 1.5m-2m. They scan, arrive, attack and hit hard. Even high levels die quickly when several winter wolves and large bucks arrive and attack at the same time. Also they loot. So if you die and corpse there, you have to ask several high levels to help with the cr.

Can this part of Tundra be reviewed?

I think the dangerous severity gap between silver worgs and winter wolves is too abrupt.

It's part of the game. Either learn the area, avoid it, or start playing differently.
