<div class="post" data-post-id="5406" data-author="Avatar" data-date="2005-04-03T12:00:57+00:00" data-subject="auction">

When you bid on an item, the web page doesn't apply your entire bid automatically. It starts at whatever the current value is, adds the increment, and applies that as your bid. If that's high enough to win the item, you don't pay your highest bid you only pay the current winning bid.

If there's an item on there that someone has bid 500 coins on, and the increment is 50 coins, you might bid 1000 but the web page will only apply 550. If someone then bids 600 the computer records their bid, but right after their bid puts the value at 600 then the computer still knows that you will pay up to 1000, so it adds the increment again and applies 650, and you're still winning even though you haven't logged onto the web page again during the other person's bid of 600.

</div>
