Hi all,

I would like to start by saying that I have quite a bit of experience in these matters.  The purpose of this post is to give a couple of suggestions designed to increase traffic in our forums.  They are purely suggestions.  In other words, it won't hurt my feelings if y'all just say "Nah."

1.Lending Library: A section dedicated to the trade of spellbooks, a place for "teachers" to advertise what they currently have available, and a place for players to post the Sbs they are looking for.

2.Looking for Group:  This section would be a nice place to organize and schedule group runs.  This would be an excellent spot for those on a work/school/life schedule to find the xp/eq/cp/etc groups they are looking for.

3.Kudos:  A spot to congratulate people on reaching Milestones, getting that special peice of Eq, or whatev. 

I would like to point out that all of these (especially #2 and #3) could also be accomplished very nicely on a Facebook fan page. I could go on, but I digress.

Thanx for reading,
