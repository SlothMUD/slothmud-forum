If you are using 3.0 and you find that some of your scripts, aliases, and triggers just mysteriously blank out, i.e. they are still listed but are blank, then you may want to revert to 2.1.

This doesn't seem to affect everyone. It is somehow related to the scrolling and selecting functions and so it depends on your specific usage and perhaps on your device. Personally it's killing me and I'm reverting to 2.1. Posting this to save anyone else the headache of figuring out what's going on.
