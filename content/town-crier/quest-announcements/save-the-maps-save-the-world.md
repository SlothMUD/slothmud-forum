Greetings, mortals.

I received today a disturbing letter from my friend, the cograph mapmaker :

> <strong>Quote:</strong>
> Salutations, old friend.
> 
> 
> 
> I am upset. Quite upset. I like maps. Those small pieces of paper hold the wisdom of the people that explored the land before us. And it seems that no-one care about them anymore.
> 
> 
> 
> I visited the other day my friend Curtis the cartographer, the assistant of the mighty Benq. I admit that i hadn't visited him in a couple years, but he looked like fifty years older. He said that no-one bring him new material anymore. Granted, everyone go there and check his maps, but no-one bring him new maps.
> 
> 
> 
> Not that long ago we had every single place of this world mapped and cataloged. But world evolve. Some places have changed, other have been recently discovered. And no-one took the effort of mapping them.
> 
> 
> 
> I would fix it myself, but im stuck in that Maw place, and I wont leave it until im done with it.
> 
> 
> 
> I beg for your help. Make the adventurers help us in this task. Tempt them, treat them, force them, I don't care. But if we don't fix this soon, the art of mapping will be lost for good, and new generations wont have any clue about where they are, or about where to go.
> 
> 
> 
> Help me, old friend. You are my only hope.
> 
> 
> 
> Sincerely yours.
> 
> The cograph mapmaker.

I like maps myself. But above it, I like to help my friends. And I wont fail the mapmaker after all we have lived together, I remember how excited he was when he first found those snakes in the Maw.

Every Sunday, I will choose an area that needs help with map. Areas will be all range, some will require a powerful adventurer to explore, others will be part of the lands of Valkyre. You guys will have seven of your mortal days (that is, until next Saturday) to send me a map of that area. To send the maps, use that spell that you guys call email with the target isabo@slothmud.org.

Things that I will use to determine how good or bad a map is :

 How complete it is. You guys can know if you completely have an area with the score.

 How exact it is. A wrong exit here and there? Bad bad.

 How helpful it is. Rooms that require water breath, fly, fire rooms, surrounding areas, things that should be know ... all counts.

 How beauty it is. Any useless uncalled stuff will actually count negatively. Its not the same making a map beautiful (check Mercurial Woods map) than trying to be annoying (check L.A.A.A.A.M. map).

Things that every single map should have :

[\*Benq Note\*] 

 \* Area Background should be <strong>transparent</strong> unless the background is a \_nice\_ picture (like Mercurial Woods)

 \* File Format should be <strong>PNG</strong>

[\*/Benq Note\*]

 Area name.

 Continent name.

 Run from the standard recall point to the entrance of the area.

 Any other useful run related to the area. For instance, the Castle of the Family DeGrance should contain the run to Acarni.

 Name of the immortal that made the area.

 (optional) Your own name.

I will check the received maps, discuss with other immortals, choose the one that we like more and send it to Curtis during the next week. The author of the map we choose will receive a reward, usually in those shiny pieces that you mortals call "drachma". Reward will vary, depending on how complex the area to map is and on how good the map is. I reserve the right to not reward anyone or reward several people, always depending on quality.

Mortal incarnation of divinities won't be able to take part in this adventure. <img src="/images/smilies/icon_twisted.svg" alt=":twisted:" title=":twisted:" width="15" height="15" class="smiley nozoom"> 

Best regards.

Pd. I had a million ideas to write down and I am prolly forgetting something, so will edit this message if I remember something important.
