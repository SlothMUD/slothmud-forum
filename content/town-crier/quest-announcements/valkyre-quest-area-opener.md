Tomorrow, we'll be opening a new area on the continent of Valkyre. There will be a little bit of

drachma, some discovery, maybe some new items indicative of the area, and some fun. We 

will likely carry the fun off of Valkyre for a little while after the new area is discovered and 

experienced as well including some drachma and epics. This is not meant to be a big quest,

we just wanted to open the new area with something more than a simple "Here you go!".

So bring your valkers if you have some to join the portion on Valkyre, and then join as

you may afterwards. 

We will experience a short reboot in the morning, and within an hour after that reboot,

we will fire it up. Reboot time will be normal time for those of us who have been around

enough to know when the automated reboot hits usually on Sundays (9:30 am server time).
