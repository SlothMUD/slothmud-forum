The Aisholm Explorer's Guild is proud to announce that after several months,

the first set of verses from a newly discovered set of lost pages referred to

as "The Songs of Hidden Snow" have finally been translated. It is with great

anticipation that they present this translation from the ancient vernacular

for your perusal. Thanks for your continued support, and we hope to see

more of you in the near future aiding with our ongoing endeavors to discover

more of our continent's lost treasures. The verses are translated as such:

----------------------------------------------------------

"It is a tale of ice and snow,

Where the Bunyips swim 

     and howling winds blow,

Where giants once stalked,

But trudge no more,

And this bard shall sing,

    Of where she did go.

There I met I met my goddess fair,

Where the bards do sing,

    And poets recite,

Where the daughters,

Of inspiration forever dwell,

And the heavens reveal,

    Land of eternal night.

There, my goddess spoke soft and firey,

And raged against me,

    With jealous surprise,

Her passion was fierce,

And her eyes were wild,

But her words were entrancing,

    As ever she was wise. "

----------------------------------------------------------

We will be holding an opener quest on Sunday June 11th between 12:00 p.m. and 1:00 p.m.

For those able to attend, please be prepared to spend a few hours from beginning to start.

This quest will be for everyone who is willing and able to join, and we only have one request

and/or requirement at this time: You'll need a Bard Prime or two. There will be drachma and

a horde of new items to discover as well as one our favorite things: a new area.

We will meet in Judge's Temple on Aisholm when it is time to start.

Hope to see you guys there!

-Neptune
