Hello Denizens of Sloth,

 Sorry for the late announcement but preparations for this years

 Christmas Quest have been

 a little more challenging than normal.  Throughout the world of Jord,

 all the Christmas Stockings have vanished.  To restore them to their

 rightful places, we must recover them from Satan Claws' demonic army

 of elves.

 The quest will be held at 7pm Server Time on Christmas Day itself, the 25th.

 Type "time start" within the game to see the server time.

 We will have two teams (and we need to start picking Captains for

 those teams right away,

 post in the thread on the Forums if you want to be a Team Captain and

 I will set up a poll at

 about noon on the 24th server time, and then voting will close on the

 poll early on Christmas

 day).

 The captains will then get alternating selection going down the who

 list in order of size, as

 to who they want on their team.

 For the winning team that reclaims the most stockings from the Elf

 army, a modest amount

 of drachma will be awarded (we went light on the size of the reward

 partly because of how

 late this is being announced).

 For the losing team, half of that reward will be given out.

 See you there,

 --Idjit
