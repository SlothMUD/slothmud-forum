Howdy.  You'll find tokens are a pretty hot topic.  Known/common pops are cycled fairly regular.  The Russians are a little more organized and have a helpful page that can help you track down some tokens:

[http://wiki.slothmud.ru/Tokens?v=8hx](http://wiki.slothmud.ru/Tokens?v=8hx)

Sloth crier also has some suggestions on where to track down tokens as well in the Tokens section I think...

I think the token pop rate was turned up recently?  If I hasn't been, it wouldn't be a bad idea with all the new folks.  :) 

Also, any smallish mob has a chance of popping a tri token, so places like Daar Tempest, Kobolds, and Grymlyns are fairly cool places to wipe out to collect tokens, though try to be polite to any newbies who are cycling the area for xp.

Happy hunting.
