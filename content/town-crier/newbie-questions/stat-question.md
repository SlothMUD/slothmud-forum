<div class="post" data-post-id="31310" data-author="Thraxas" data-date="2010-06-17T16:37:43+00:00" data-subject="Re: Stat question">

18 is the max a stat can be without maxstat equipment

help maxstat gives more information about getting stats above 18, but suffice to say in the normal run of equipment (i.e. excepting runes {help gem / help rune} and excepting drachma equipment {help drachma} and excepting triple40 equipment {http://www.slothmud.org/index.php?module=Pages&func=display&pageid=16}) 18 is the maximum you'll get in any stat.
