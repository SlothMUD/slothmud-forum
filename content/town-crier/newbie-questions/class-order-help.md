<div class="post" data-post-id="18852" data-author="Giselle" data-date="2007-06-12T06:19:28+00:00" data-subject="">

The positioning of cleric in your classo will affect the range of supplicants at your disposal. Have a look at the helpfile for the full list and the level restricts on each.

Ne/Dr = Great mregen and fluidity spell but note you can't order your undeads while shifted.

Mo/Ne vs Ne/Mo - Having a non-caster class as second will hurt your mregen but it works for some people. That said, as a necro you consume a chunk of mana to summon and prep your pets. Regen gets tedious <img src="/images/smilies/icon_e_wink.svg" alt=";)" title=";)" width="15" height="15" class="smiley nozoom">

Also, the order lag will be shorter for a Necro prime.

I haven't played a tank in ages but that classo is pretty popular for tanks because of wraithform and spectral shield. Having pets will boost your solo capability.
