mansion mobs hit hard but are very thin, easy for mages with mirrors (when i run it with my mage prime i don't usually bother with sanc), and easy for monks, but they are nostab. if you lose con, or whiff your grip, you've got a good chance of dying. all xp areas are like that (well, the good ones).

rogue of storms will generally take 2 mid-sized avatars, although i'm sure there are people who solo it (\*cough\* necros \*cough\*).

winds are all not too bad, just thick - very soloable at 3x40 if you have decent gear, or just bring a friend, and you can kill all of them 2man.

about 1.5-2m xps, from what i recall

morrigan is biggish - couple good avatars, or a group (again, it's also solo for some, but i'm assuming you're not asking about what you can do after you get 3k drach in eq).

it's been so long since i killed him i have no clue how much xps he's worth now, sorry

Dragoons are easy stab mobs - splorkable for a thief with decent eq, altho i'd imagine they're a bitch if you try to fight them straight.

1m-ish each, depending on how long they've been up.

as to beacons in castle black, i don't believe that you can beacon there, but really i can't recall ever trying to.

the color-robed lords/ladies in castle black are good, common newbie gold mobs too - nothing spectacular but not bad.
