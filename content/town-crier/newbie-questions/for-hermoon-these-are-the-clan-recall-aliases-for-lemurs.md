{SS2AH} --\> {krr;enter silver}

{SS2BH} --\> {krr;enter golden}

{SS2IS} --\> {krr;enter crimson}

S.S. is the safe ones, and the colored portals are right there.

this is if you krr from the Well of Settlestone, (or anywhere on SS)

{AH2BH} --\> {krr;west;en mirror;en bh}

{AH2IS} --\> {krr;west;en mirror;en island}

{AH2SS} --\> {krr;west;en mirror;en ss}

A.H. is the one with the imp. this is if you krr from Wide Gate (or anywhere on AH)

krr on BH takes you to dragon mountain. (technically draconian Lair but if you open secret here you can go up to the Center of the Mountain)

krr on Lyme takes you to the Dank Air Bubble where you can Beg Mind to create a gate from Seamus (the innkeeper) to Poseidon's realm.

Thanks for all input. 

I think newbies and lowbies should take note that some of these recalls are dangerous and/or problematic: 

1\) you can be transported to a room where you are attacked - make sure you have full HP and recall quickly so that you can safely escape to the recall point on the destination continent/island,

2\) you can find yourself unable to pass an aggro mob (imp) - make sure you recall quickly - alas you will be transported back to the continent/island on which you attempted to use the clan recall.

It begs the question why the 'newbie' clan has clan recalls that present such challenges to low level characters...
