<div class="post" data-post-id="3314" data-author="12345" data-date="2004-12-14T18:57:16+00:00">

As a rule, you must cross an integer, however there is another consideration to this.  Web affects your AC by +.3, so many will will run with an AC of -7.3, -8.3, etc so their AC doesn't change when webbed.

My understanding is that only the integers make a difference.

However, it is fairly common for tank-types to run 0.3 over the integer to keep themselves at an integer AC in case of a web.  (Webs reduces AC by 0.3.)
