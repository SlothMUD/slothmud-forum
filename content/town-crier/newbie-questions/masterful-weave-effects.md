<div class="post" data-post-id="1919" data-author="Hotara" data-date="2004-09-13T13:32:16+00:00">

Masterful Aegis : Increase In Duration of Spell

Masterful Sanctuary : Increase In Duration of Spell

Normal Undead Visage : Your skin shrivels and sags as you take on a hideous undead visage.

Masterful Undead Visage : Your skin becomes beautifully pale as you take on an elegant undead visage.

*Not sure on effect here, but i assume the % of you getting detected by undead being is less.

Masterful Stamina : At Avatar 40, gives 75 hp instead of the default 50.

Masterful Locate : Gives the Continent of where the item worm is in.

Masterful Vision : A vision will show you not only the location of the player, but also all of his equipment, and inventory.

Masterful Create Food : Kind of like produces interesting food, these food are rather heavy, and fulfilling.

Masterful Dispel Magic : Shows the spell that you have dispelled off of a mob.

Masterful Curse : Curses, etc, and also burns the mob, i think.

Masterful Word of Recall : Able to recall while being demon bound {Status ailment where unable to recall}

Masterful Relocation : Able to relocate to a dead player who is gateable.

Masterful Raise Dead : 100% Success {Not Tested, But Never Seen Failure}, and raises with more Hp than 1... around 200 + hp gained when max hp was 681. {Again, not sure if MAX HP or LEVEL affects this value}

Masterful Acid Blast : Blinds Mob for 1 tick.

Might Have errors.
