<div class="post" data-post-id="4435" data-author="blackmore" data-date="2005-02-14T02:50:33+00:00" data-subject="">

I thought I'd replied to this thread once, but it must have been lost.

Please note that the restrictions are based on mortal estimates, and have never been officially confirmed.  They are extremely close, but the cutoff between newbie and appie, and between appie and adept are more rough estimates.  This is mostly because I could never find any equipment with the right level restrictions to know for certain.  The cutoffs for adept-exp, exp-master and master-supreme are definite.

The * in the eqlist means that when a mass restriction update was done, those restricts had been verified as accurate.  The restricts might have been changed since then, nobody asks my permission before they change restricts on an item, but at one time, they were verified and they did not include any new class restricts.

</div>
