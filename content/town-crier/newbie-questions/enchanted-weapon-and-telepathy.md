<div class="post" data-post-id="31334" data-author="Atwell" data-date="2010-06-28T22:05:08+00:00">

Hi Towen,

1. Yes; enchant weapon gives +1hit,+1dam to a weapon and gives it the MAGIC flag.  At higher levels, it will give +2hit,+2dam.  Enchant weapon only works on weapons that have no affects at present (only damage dice).  If you query about the weapon in a shop and see AFFECTS anywhere, you wouldn't be able to enchant it.  Detect magic is a spell that affects you generally; you don't cast it on a specific item.  If you cast detect magic, you should see that your enchanted sword "glows violet" (this is the MAGIC flag that it gained).

2. I don't think looking at a mob would improve telepathy.  Relatively few mobs ever have thoughts visible to telepathy, but when they do, you just need to stand at the mob for a brief time to hear them.
