<div class="post" data-post-id="12913" data-author="12345" data-date="2006-06-08T04:02:38+00:00" data-subject="">

The biggest thing holding you back is AC.  If you can, roll until you have at least 16 dex with that classo.  If you run kobolds and goblin miners, you should be able to pop some tri tokens to roll with.  Get some quickling bracers or other +dex gear to get to 18 dex.  You should be able to find quite a bit of good AC eq for your level in the shops on Valk and BH.  If you've been running Blaster's temple, you should have a few extra coins to help you with that.

Greenish rings, mystic plate mail, green crested shields, lesser helm, silver leg plates, chainmail sleeves and such are some decent eq and will help your AC quite a bit.  You should be able to get -6 with armor spell pretty easy.  Once you have curse and web spells, you should be able to carve up the masters in Star City pretty easy.  You'll need fly spell, detect invis, and recall scrolls.  You can also buy moldy scrolls in town to web with if you don't have web spell.

Once you get stone skin and -7, you'll be a lot more tankish and can start chopping down mobs like Ersa and Roach in small groups with a cleric and and a blinder.
