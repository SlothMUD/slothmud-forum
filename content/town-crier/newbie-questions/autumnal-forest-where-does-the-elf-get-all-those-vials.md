Elf isn't broken, he is designed to do what he does.

If you want to kill him you got to keep him down...

Trip/Bash him.

When he can't get up, fireball him.

Write some scripts to "scan" for him and run to him when he phases.

As long as you trip/bash he's not very hard... just a pain.
