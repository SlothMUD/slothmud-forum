I've been playing this mud for 3 weeks now and am L20 N/D/C/B. I thought I'd make sure that anyone else just joining on doesn't miss out on this fantastic mana saving feature for spellcasters with mage oriented spells.

When in the mud, type help memorize. Then get your butt to BH and pick up some ioun stones as soon as you can.

Any class with a mage-blast spell can memorize up to 3 spells ahead of time for you to 'bank' your mana. Very useful stuff. And the stones are reusable! So anytime your gleader says 'regen', pop those stones out and start memorizing for the next run!

Now, I've tried memorizing 'curse', which is also a mage spell, but it doesn't seem to take. I'd like to know if there are any non-blasting type spells that can be memorized, the higher the mana cost the better!

GLuck to you noobs out there!

Widow
