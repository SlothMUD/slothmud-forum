<div class="post" data-post-id="8266" data-author="blackmore" data-date="2005-08-12T10:25:34+00:00">

I don't think it's a bad classo.  I'm not sure how powerful monk prime is compared to monk in other slots, but any prime is fine if that's what you decide you want to play.  The prime skill that I can think of is being able to grip ether mobs, which plays in well with mage.

As for your other classes, clerics and necros have spells that are affected based on where they are in your classo, whereas mage doesn't.  You can get more spell bonus eq with mage second, but there's no other advantage to being mage second compared to mage fourth.  Cleric has supplicants, and necros have summon undead, both of which are stronger the higher the class is in your classo.  If you don't care and just want to blast are hard as you can as quick as you can, then go for it.

<div class="post" data-post-id="8270" data-author="blackmore" data-date="2005-08-12T11:36:35+00:00">

I forgot, you asked about pvp, 'splork' mobs and 'chop' mobs.

pvp happens at most once a year, in the arena, for very nice prizes.  It isn't worth creating a character specifically for that purpose.

'splork' mobs refer to any mob that dies on a stab or grip.  In generally, referring to a mob as a splork means it has few enough hps that you should kill it right away.

A chop mob is a mob on lyme that is insanely thick.  There are chop weapons on lyme that cause roughly 1/7th to 1/10th of the mob's max hps when the weapon procs.  The group doesn't blast, you just blind and heal the tank.  It's become less common with changes to xp rates.  People frequently refer to a mob being a 'chop mob' if it's thick with little hps, because the group will hack it down without much blasting.  In that sense, monk prime is one of the best because you can strike with no cost.
