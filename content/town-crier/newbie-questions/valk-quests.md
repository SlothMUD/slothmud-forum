<div class="post" data-post-id="30626" data-author="*juggleblood*" data-date="2010-01-10T23:37:55+00:00" data-subject="">

ask mobname about quest

A questmob tells you 'I offer the quest goldfish for 1 drachma.'

ask questmob about goldfish

A questmob tells you 'I've always wanted a pet goldfish. Can you get me one?'

undertake goldfish

A questmob tells you 'I hear they have goldfishes at the fish store. Hurry back.'

........

The query command is similar. It is used in stores and by forge mobs.

query mobname about sword
