You can only obtain 1 holy item at a time.  You must drop one before you can pray for another.

There is only 1 instance of each item per diety ingame at a time.  So if someone has  Splork's sword, that is the only Splork's sword that can exist at any time.  However, someone else could have Blaster's sword.

Yes, if someone with more sac points than you prays for the item you are currently using, then it will go poof on you regardless of what you are doing at the time.
