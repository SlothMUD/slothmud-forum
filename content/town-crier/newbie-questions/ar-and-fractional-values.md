2) If #1 is true, then fractional values for AR are not important. In other words, an AR of -2.0 and -2.9 are equal for "To Hit" rolls. Is this true?

Obviously this is only my feelings on the subject.  I could be wrong.

1.  Since all # of faces, and # of dice are integers, im not sure how you could possibly roll a non-integer number... that said i'm pretty sure that only integers are used for all rolls.  

2.  AC is only a factor in whole numbers.  So -2.9 is indeed the same as -2.1.  For that reason, you will often be looking to give away a couple points of AC in exchange for other modifiers.  However, one thing to keep in mind is this:  getting webbed costs you .3 ac.  So if you run at -3.0 ac and get webbed, you are fighting at -2.7 ac, which could be an issue.  For this reason, you will notice that some players choose to run at -X.3 AC.

3.  I cannot think of anything else that is effected by AC.
