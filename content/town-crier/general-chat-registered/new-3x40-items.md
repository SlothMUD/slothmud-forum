Thought I'd post my first impressions of the new system...

* Firstly there is no disadvantages with this new system at all, because the current system remains and new options and power have been created. Thanks to the imms for organising this, as it's gone a long ways to fixing gaps in the system with older problems like the absence of stats like Undead Control, Spells Saves, Hand Dam etc.

* As you progress through the game you basically get special tokens now...

3x40 earns 1st token

6x40 earns 2nd token

8x40 earns 3rd token

Rebirth + 3x40 earns 4th token

Rebirth + 6x40 earns 5th token

Rebirth + 8x40 earns 6th token

Tokens are spent on items worth, 1 or 2 (6x40 item) or 3 (8x40 item) tokens. The more tokens the item is worth, the better it is. You can use these tokens to spend on combinations... any number of combinations of the 3/6/8x40 items as long as you have the tokens to spend. For example a rebirth 8x40 could have 2 8x40 items or 3 6x40 items, 6 3x40items or combinations like 1 8x40 item, 1 6x40 item and 1 3x40 item etc. In each of these cases the sum worth of all the items is 6 tokens.

* Advanced players who have eq modes, where nearly every item changes between eq modes have a lot of options to look at, but quick analysis reveals that the clasps might be good options for these players as they can be worn in every mode... not just eq you wear in one mode.
