I understand your comment. However that information often doesn't mean very much, and a lot of that information was wrong in my opinion. Typically areas have both small, high solo and group mobs. So how do you say?

Xp areas - in a general sense go something like this for new players... 

VK Town (lvl < 10

Blasters (lvl < 20)

Catacombs (20+)

Murkwood (35+)

Shadow-keep (40)

Lyme etc (3x40)

More Class Dependant Areas...

Mage Tower Ruins 40+ (Warrior)

Sheol 40+ (Warrior)

Sakura 30+ (Warrior/Monk)

Dark Dreams 35+ (Warrior/Cleric)

Masion 38+ (Mage)

Koz 35+ (Thief)

Infirm 40+ (Thief)

Hive 25+ (Monk)

This is a rule of thumb, not the case for every player. XP is mostly about finding what works for you. The road between 2x40 and 4x40 is real slow. The xp required becomes very high, but your power doesn't increase much, it's more your stability/fortitude as a character that increases.

The real experienced players you won't find in these areas for long, if at all... mainly because they kill a few mobs in a lot of areas for better xp yield. Kill history sucks, and punishes the newer players the most. The players who die exploring will always end up becoming better players for it... (Although some don't seem to learn) There is no substitute in this game for exploring - interestingly enough it's often the least thing that is done. XP and areas is such an individual thing it's pretty hard to "generalize."

Maybe don't look at "areas" in regards to difficulty... look at "mobs" individually.

Clear as mud?
