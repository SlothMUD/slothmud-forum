tldr: it's hard to increase mob exp by only increasing their dam, so they boost their hps, with several Lyme exceptions.

You'll notice that:

- large soft-shelled turtles

- patriarch/matriarch in necrop

- firemage in necrop

- apparition in necrop

- terracotta warriors/sentries

- the eye in UD2

are killed much easier, than most offlyme mobs.

Why?

Very roughly, Exp = hps * K * dam * procs/L*gold.

Those mobs are thin, but hit hard.

More mobs:

lizardmen

ogres

baboons

spiders in M&M.

They hit 700 per round easily, can be killed in 0-1 rounds with 3 people for 10-20mil total.

That damage is the difference between the exp, with hps being relatively similar to the hate-filled soldiers (or even much thinner in case of spiders).

After 700dam/round you probably shouldn't increase their dam, so imms increase hps, which turns a 5mil into a 10mil mob and a 10mil mob into a 30mil group mob.

I *think* that reducing mob hitroll and boosting damage simultaneously might work - but I think "walkers" will consider this cheating to boost exp.

Example: djinnis in the fire extension are 7-8mil. They don't hit much against -14ac despite having moderate damage, but they are thick and have at least 1 proc. Using ice softens them up, but after a few you need to regen anyway, because there are only 3-4 of them.

Another point, which you might've noticed, is gold. It reduces mob exp by a disproportionate amount. For exp, I'd prefer a 10mil mob with 1 coin than a 8mil mob with 10k gold - and even then I'm not sure the 8m mob would be easier to kill. e.g., compare terracotta warriors with the sahaguin temple guards.

I know you'd prefer the latter, though - but then, why would you complain about mobs being low exp for the effort?

Also, procs. They influence exp somehow, and mobs w/o procs or spells/immunes are just less exp. Aggro mobs should increase exp as well.

If I were to make avatar-solo mobs, I'd copy terracotta warriors or eye of the deep and boost their dam by 30-50%. Maybe tweak hitroll a bit, add some dispel and vorpal procs. j/k about procs, but you get the drift.
