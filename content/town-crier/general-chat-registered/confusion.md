Well, I'm all in favor of having confusion code.  There are definitely certain mobs who you would expect could hit you with a confusion ability.

I have a big issue with how locate object works - I always have.  Non upper level mage primes and non avatars can locate on something like "black-raft" and get a list of leather items, none of which are rafts, because of the way locate object works (it will return the list of items that match the first keyword in the most recent black-raft to enter the game - so if the keywords are leather-black-raft, a list of leather items is returned).  I know this has been shot down before, but I don't understand how asking for one list and getting a completely separate list is anything other than a bug.

I dislike the idea simply because it's difficult not to locate too much for most players, depending on what they're locating for.

ps - my explanation of how locate works is purely from experimentation about 5 years ago, if I'm wrong, I don't care.
