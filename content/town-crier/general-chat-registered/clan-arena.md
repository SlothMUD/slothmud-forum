I've got something like this on the drawing board, but haven't built it on the builders port yet.

Its a chaos arena with 2 bases, light and dark, separated by a short playing field. I've got a drawing on paper, but no scanner.

Each base will have one very thick mob in the center and you win by killing the mob in the enemy's base. Each base has three entrances with large guardian mobs that block the opposing team (can't flee or hunt pass).  Guardian mobs don't respawn.

The base mob and guardian mobs are alignment aggro. So before the match the light team must all get aligned good and the dark team aligned evil. It'd be easy enuff to make a referee type mob that sets player alignments automatically at start of match.

Also each base contains a store where they can purchase healing potions, dispel grenades, and troops. Purchasing troops causes alignment aggro wandering mobs to appear in the center of the board. So lets say I'm on the good team and I purchase "treant wave". A bunch of wandering treants appear in the center of the board that will only be aggro to the evil players.

Death: dead players remain where they lie. You're free to cast web on them, push them into your own base, whatever. But Corpses are sacrosanct and may only be looted or pick up by their owner. This is enforced by proc, you simply won't be able to break this rule. Also after a short amount of time, corpses will teleport to the center of the board. (so your corpse won't be trapped in the enemy base) 

Money: players must deposit all gold before they enter the arena. There will be an entry way with a referee and bank teller available to do this. After they enter the arena, the agreed upon amount of gold will be distributed to each player. Very small amounts of gold will be very significant in this arena.

Timed events: if the match starts dragging on, certain random events can occur designed to speed up an outcome. For example: arena wide dispel, large aggro mob spawn, weakening of guardians,  etc..

I based this loosely on Hero Wars or Dota from WC3 and had clan contests in mind.
