> 
> \-- Splork

I have a more extensive post prepared which goes into detail about each level 40, pet options, etc. But it turned into a (much bigger) wall of text that few would be interested in. Here's the important point:

As a necro secondary, progression coming from "summoning the best available pets by... level" is not viable. I was disheartened upon reaching 4x40 and discovering I could only animate a single corpse. I was devastated upon talking to an Arch-Lich, and finding out that 3x wraiths/vampires (available at 3x40/2x40) will be my best pet option all the way to 9x40.

While the concept for non-primes sounds fine, the fact that you can never summon enough 'best available' pets to overcome the quantity of wraiths/vampires at 3x40 pets breaks the entire progression.

There are two obvious solutions: 

1\) NERF! If non-primes could only summon two wraiths, the entire 'lack of progression' issue vanishes. Upon learning control horde, a nonprime can summon two of the 'best available pet', which will be better than <em>two</em> wraiths. It is the quantity of low level pets that ruins the progression.

2\) Buff! Allow non-primes to increase their bonus servant points, based on the position of necro in their classo. Knowing that eventually 3x vilewights, bonesnarls, banshees, or phantoms will be available is a strong incentive to keep up with the xp grind. 3x40 to 9x40 is a very long way, and the feeling of progression is what keeps us in the hunt.

Having played a necro prime to avatar on s3, I feel that the necromancer's supremacy in pet control is represented very well by the Juju zombie. They don't need exclusive servant points to make the prime a popular and viable option.

In my opinion, the 'buff' option is better in this situation. The possible addition of a third high level pet for non-primes is an important balance decision, so please leave some feedback everyone!
