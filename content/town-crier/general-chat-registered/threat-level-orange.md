<div class="post" data-post-id="16816" data-author="Firon" data-date="2007-02-11T23:59:44+00:00" data-subject="Area Opener - Kingdom of Salamis">

My report on things  :D 

As was mentioned, Juggleblood held his opener today for Kingdom of Salamis.

It started out on Valkyre with Demogorgon attacking Newbatia, Mayor Galio of Bal Harbour being abducted and Osama himself making threats. Demogorgon was eventually dispatched by an intrepid group of valkers with the help of Colonel "I know nuthink!" Clink to be the meatshield of the group.

Looted from the corpse of Demo were some gold bars of Dwarvish make. Off we went to Settlestone to see if we could find out where the gold came from to hire Demo to attack Newbatia!

Once there, several fruitless forays were made by the group floundering about SS looking for the mayor. Truth made her way to King Brandor in Dwarven Mountain and discovered that he had something to say to the group. It turns out that thieves had snuck by his vault guardian and looted the gold that was used to pay Demogorgon to do his dirty deed. In return for giving the gold back to him, King Brandor offered the services of one of his dwarvish scouts to track down the brigands who stole it.

The dwarf in question is Chopak (located 8s6w3s2es of well) who resides in the SS Monk Guild that is part of Dwarven Mountain. In return for a large sum of money (greatly decreased now that quest is over) he showed us to the Kingdom of Salamis!

Once there we wandered about a bit trying to figure out how to get to Osama and free Mayor Galio. Eventually we made our way there past lascivious camels, unruly mobs and even martyrs-in-training who blew us up!

We eventually killed Osama, Al'Zuwahiri, freed Galio to save the day. Then with some hints we found our way to the big mob, Iblis, which was dispatched and swag was received.

Big thanks to Juggleblood and Clink for running opener, Marvel and Isaiah for leading us unruly punks and everyone who came and went during the quest to help out!
