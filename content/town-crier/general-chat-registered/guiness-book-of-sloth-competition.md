I've decided to try holding a fun (?) competition over the course of the next week in a bid to raise the collective knowledge of mobs and areas. The competition has two categories...

Category 1: Most Kills in 30mins

Category 2: Most Coins in 30mins

Rule 1) Players must reset "counters" and use only solo counters to start their entry.

Rule 2) Solo counters most be posted in this thread at the end of 30mins to register your score. 

Rule 3) You can post updates to your scores in this thread if you improve.

Rule 4) All mobs killed in the competition must be of full health before killing.

Rule 5) Players must log there 30mins of play in case the results are called into question.

Rule 6) Players must respect the spirit of which the competition is offered... there are many ways to cheat... don't...

The prizes for each category are 25 random T1 gems, and 2.5m gold coins for the player to roll at the next Reece, a planar page from EU and 4 lutefisk.
