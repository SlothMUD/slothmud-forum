<div class="post" data-post-id="32909" data-author="Skyv" data-date="2011-09-14T15:06:49+00:00">

Hi all,  just wanted to toss out another type of idea for spending my well (or poorly) earned drachma.  I was wondering whether we could get an actual blacksmith type mob set up that would be able to build custome equipment for each person.  I realize this could get very ugly trying to balance all of the equipment slots in terms of costs/benefits, so maybe to start with, a clasp builder.  This idea might overlap too much with avatar lights/9x40 avatar bonus purchases, and I'm sure that's where the idea came from (creativity = not my strong suit), but this might provide some interim customization for people who are still a long way away from 9x40 (which is most of us).  

My thought is that for say 200 drachma, you can buy a black clasp template with a custom name and the attachment spot of your choosing.  You could then bring it back to the blacksmith, and for extra drachma add in bonuses.  Limit the number of clasps that can be built (maybe only allow 1 per character).  I would also scale the costs of the bonuses in terms of the bonus itself (max_stat > stat > regen > spell effect) as well as the number of bonuses already applied to the clasp (say double the cost for each additional bonus).  I would suggest that these scale these similar to the avatar bonus list so that there aren't conflicts.  There could also be limits to how many of each bonus you could put on (say only one max_stat per clasp)

As an example, and using the avatar shop costs as the baseline, lets say for the clasp bonus costs were 100 drachma per 10million xp cost in the shop listing.  In other words, 0.1 ac would costs 300, max stats would cost 200, regular stats would cost 100, etc.  I could then buy a template for 200 drachma, decide I wanted some max_dex on it, which would cost me an additional 200.  Maybe I still had drachma burning a hole in my pocket and I wanted some more hand damage.  The base price for hand damage would be 50, but because it is the second slot, it would cost me 100.  If I wanted some more, the next point of hand damage would cost me 200, etc.

Just an idea to throw out there

-Skyv
