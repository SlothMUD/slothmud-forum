<div class="post" data-post-id="17255" data-author="Avatar" data-date="2007-03-12T00:37:28+00:00" data-subject="Hitroll">

As you mention, there's a lot of hitroll equipment in game and it's visible in score. That is enough to actually track the numbers and find out for ourselves what difference +hit makes. Imms here rarely spell things out for us, and it would probably bore us if they did.

I'd suggest running a small set of mobs and tracking those stabs separately rather than hitting a normal stab run and tracking. Try to pick something that you don't hit 95% during normal combat. Get about 100 stabs and track (whiff whiff, whiff stab, stab stab) with the lowest +hit you can achieve. Then add the +hit eq and see what the difference is on another 100 attempts. 

Above all, don't trust your perceptions. It's easy to hit 45 stabs and miss the last 3 then believe that your stab rate sucks. FYI, the last time I tracked stabs with a Thief prime, I adjusted by +12 to hit through eq and didn't record any difference in the stabs. That's been years ago, and I don't have the details any more.
