<div class="post" data-post-id="10320" data-author="blackmore" data-date="2006-01-24T22:09:20+00:00">

Focus does quite a bit more in terms of adding damage than gods does.  However, brothers makes your stabs hit much more consistently, so monk increases your max xp per run while brothers decreases your min.  Between the 2, what I took would just depend on the other 2 classes.

If I were making a monster stab char, I would take monk and bard both and do w/out mage.  You can blast some with bard and no charisma eq, same as you can blast some w/cleric, or you can use circle or get really tricky and remove your weapon to strike.  They don't make up for the blasting that a mage gets, but your goal is to bring down the biggest mob possible with your stab, not to have to blast hard after stabbing.
