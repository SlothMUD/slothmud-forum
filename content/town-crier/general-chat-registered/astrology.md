The way it works currently is that if there's a minor conjunction and a major conjunction happens in the middle of it, the reading counts that as part of the length of the minor. This is to reduce the verbiage.

So:

at +33 minutes: minor conjunction starts

at +88 minutes: minor conjunction turns into major conjunction

at +117 minutes: major conjunction turns back into minor conjunction

at +188 minutes: minor conjunction ends

I would be keen to hear suggestions for better ways to display this information.
