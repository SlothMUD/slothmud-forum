<div class="post" data-post-id="39172" data-author="*Neptune*" data-date="2019-04-17T00:27:08+00:00" data-subject="Re: Do Suck Rooms Count Towards Exploration?">

Those rooms shouldn't affect your exploration unless you enter that room right at the point when

the proc pulses. I did, however, notice one errant room that was approved and put in game as an

oversight in that zone. I have since removed that one room from the game as there's nothing

there and you wouldn't be able to reach it anyways without a very lucky teleport spell. That

room shouldn't detract from your exploration anymore. 

I'll check some further in case there's something unforeseen about those triggers that we

didn't anticipate when they were implemented, however.
