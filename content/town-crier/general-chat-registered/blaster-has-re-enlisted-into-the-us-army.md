Hey all,

Have an update on my army enlistment.  I finally got everything set and signed up this last Friday.  The experience was two days of being wore out and tired as hell.  In the end I didn't get anything for bonuses due to being prior service and the army not giving much of a crap about us prior people, but yet they whine they are hurting for people, go figure.

I did however get station of choice but not like I had much to pick from since only two locations came up.  Fort Sill, Oklahoma, and Hawaii.  Guess what one I picked?  That's right, I am going to Hawaii.

It seems the army wants everyone fairly bad due to my departure date.  I was hoping not to leave before late August or early September, but instead I have less then 2 weeks to get my stuff squared away and report.  That's right, I have to report back to the MEPPS station at 6am July 21, 2005.  Not much time, so I will be seen on the game very little since I have a great deal to do to get ready.

The good news is, I will still be active, for awhile it might only be via email until I have everything settled over in Hawaii, but I do plan on staying active.  Also the unit I will be going to has just returned this year from Afghanistan so it is less likely that I will be heading out to the war right away, but still possible.

Lastly, This last Tuesday I went ahead and got engaged to my girlfriend, some of you know her as Charsi from the game.  We have been together just under 2 years now and will be married on July 18th.  Only a few days before I leave, but once I get settled over in Hawaii, she will be joining me and I am sure you will see both of us on the mud together once again.  

Overall, I have a 3 year contract, and do plan on staying in at this point of the game, but also will have a 3 year tour in Hawaii, arn't I so lucky to get one of the nicer places to go?  I thought so.

Anyway, as I said, I plan on being active still so don't disown me yet.   

:D
