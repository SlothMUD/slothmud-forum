All I can say is Wow.  My power went out at 1pm on Friday and it just came up about 3 hours ago.  The internet just came online minutes ago.  I have a pretty cool picture of the 2 transformers on my block laying in the street in front of my house.  I have seen at least 5 houses crushed by large oak trees that uprooted and left 7 foot tall root/dirt mounds.  The corporate hanger at the airport lost its roof and insulation along with pizza hut, dollar general among other places.  My giant sycamore(100+') didn't lose a limb.  I did lose a few shingles and a piece of siding.  Had to clear out some limbs from a maple tree in the front yard that snapped in two but other than that I was fine.  Bought a 5kW generator(briggs&stratton single)  to keep my food cold and some essential stuff going.

Ameren did a great job.  I went outside every so often and watched them really busting ass to get everyone back up.
