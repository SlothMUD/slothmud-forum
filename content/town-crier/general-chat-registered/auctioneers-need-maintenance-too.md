<div class="post" data-post-id="32667" data-author="victory" data-date="2011-06-11T03:14:42+00:00">

The auctioneer is a nice convenience, but it has some non-technical issues.

Primarily, my gripe is that if I bid on something early, I can't rebid, so someone can just put in a couple of extra coins a few seconds before the auction ends and there is nothing you can do about it. This is a classic ebay issue.

In general, there are 2 options for using it; do a direct buyout if the price is reasonable, or wait until last minute or so to bid, or just bid out of laziness and assume you won't get the item.

Suggestions for improvement

1. Offer hidden bids that automatically increment as others bid, up to a max bid.

2. Extend auctions for 1/2/5 minutes after the last bid made. This would make it quite similar to the old auction channel. Combine this with short auction options like 10 minutes, so you get near-instant gratification.

3. Have the auctioneer advertise using the auction channel with the auction status and periodic bids. People can always shut off if annoying using the existing command.

Just a few thoughts...

Mornin' folks,

Plenty of unseen circumstance ran interference (largely in the form of a bazillion hours of overtime), but finally got around to patching the auctioneers.

Not enough time to implement everything that I wanted to sadly, but did get one or two things in.

New in version 0.6:

- It is now possible to consign multiple items in one listing (e.g. 3 hex tokens in one listing, up to a limit of probably 10).  Format is generally consign 3 hex etc.

- Various bug fixes, you know who you are!

- Increased speed (no more 4 second lag when typing lots, hopefully)

- Won't eat items once it hits the buffer limit (shame really, they were delicious!)

- Commission fee probably fixed?  

- New name and description!  Very important fix.  No self-aggrandizing there at all! *wink*

Still to be done:

- making charms salable

- making items with runes attached salable (currently it'll detach the rune and eat it.  I don't recommend doing so!)

- extending auction time when someone bids at the last second (a la Victory's suggestion)

- better alert system for overbids/wins

Maybe will be done:

- having half day auctions/quickfire ones.  need to figure out syntax for this

- auction advertising.  how much spam is too much spam?  

Probably won't be done (due to can't figure out how/probably not possible)

- sorting the list

- hidden bids a la ebay (would require a fair amount of rewrite)

No time to debug the chap properly, so please bear with me if they exhibit some funny behavior (not of the "ha ha variety", but of the "target chobbold" one).  

Let me know what I forgot/anything else you want to see implemented.

/whee

Chobbs
