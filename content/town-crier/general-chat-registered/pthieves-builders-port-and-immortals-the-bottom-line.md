In reply to the debate taking place over pthieving immts, allow me to clear this up for everyone.  Immortals on the active game port are held to rules preventing them from participating in suspect or unacceptable behavior due to the fact that they have access to live character information and live area information.  Builders on the builder's port are not held to these same strict guidelines because the area samples provided on the builder's port are old files with little up-to-date information and NO active mortal players to spy on.  Very few, if any, builders become active immortals on game site.  Those that do are held to the same principles as any other immortal.  One of our guidelines in determining whether anyone (builder or active mortal) is allowed to become an immortal on the game site is their behavior throughout their gaming career.  As such, a known past pthief has very little chance of becoming an immortal, and a current, active pthief has no chance of becoming an immortal on game site. 

The builder's port was designed to give players with questionable pasts, and players who do not want the responsiblity of being a fulltime immortal, the chance to contribute something positive to the future of Sloth.  However, builders whose behavior on game site is excessively abusive or harassing to the quality of game play for everyone else are also subject to losing their builder privileges.  Each case is handled on a case-by-case basis in these situations.  Each and every person who is willing - and able - to put forth an effort to enhance some aspect of the game is afforded the opportunity to do so on the builder's port.  

 

Regarding pkilling pthieves.  The decision to place a temporary hiatous on pthieving might become permanent, but there has been no decision as of yet to make it a permanent restriction.  If the decision to reinstate allowed pthieving is made, I'm almsot positive these "retired" pthieves would once again become active.  As such, we have imposed no such ban on pkilling known pthieves as of the current time.  A player who has pthieving characters who wishes to play legitimately now is afforded the chance to make a new character, or use a character that is not known as a pthief and will be protected by the same rules as any other character.  In other words, if Bo and Luke are the same player, but Bo is a pthief and Luke isn't... anyone caught pkilling Luke will be subject to the same punishment as any other illegal pkilling, Bo, on the other hand, is still fair game.  If the ruling on pthieving changes (becomes permanent or otherwise), a decision will be made at that time regarding how to treat such situations. 

Akasha
