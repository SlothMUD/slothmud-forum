I recently fixed a bug that allowed necromancers to animate any size creature with any size corpse. Ive known this was a bug for quite a long time just did not have the time or energy to track it down because of other work we had been concentrating on. But because of the fix players need to find higher level corpses again, which brings me to the following. 

It has now been brought to my attention by Thraxas and confirmed by other players that a few mobs that used to be level 41 are no longer that level. The changes did not happen during this version of sloth and I cant track when they happened. I am baffled to be honest because I am unaware of how or when they change and I obviously pride myself being extremely informed about the game. We changed very few areas and mobs during the switchover and some of these mobs may of been hit. 

If you come across a mob you feel should be level 41 please enter it in the bugs list and I will check them as I come across them. If they are worthy of being a level 41 I will gladly change them. Keep in mind that upping the levels of mobs will increase the power of that mob.

I also wanted to apologize to thraxas for disagreeing with him, not listening very well, and not really discussing the matter. My vikings had just tossed an interception and I was extremely annoyed:)

Splork
