I can name at least 3 immortals who are active players though?

But yes, sometimes the pop rate on things can get really annoyingly long, especially on mobs that are difficult and need a sizeable group.

But on the other side of the spectrum, if eq did pop easily, I wouldn't exactly know how to appreciate its value. Having a lousy pop rate might make me work a long time to earn it. However, how much \_Effort\_ is needed is rather subjective in this case, and your threshold will not be the same as mine.

That said, a middle ground is not easy to achieve. It's not a matter of improving pop rates, it's just a matter of getting that lucky die roll. You'd have mobs pop in first few kills or one that won't pop for weeks on end, that's how it works. Perhaps as more areas come in, we have mobs poping equipment that are equally viable.

Take my latest predicament. I seek a -.6 held low. Sifting through the eq list shows 4 possible choice, 3 of which are avatar restricted (if not nerfed) and one more poping on Marilith which has been severely reconstructed to be formidable with a -12 tank on wraithform. Naturally this mob is not possible anytime in the near future. It would be nice if there was a middle ground where i can gain a -.6 held low that had a penalty like -int for the time being (think belt of the damned from Abbadon, Rusty Iron Bracer from greater demon). Later when it is possible, i can try my luck with Iblis and/or Marilith/Amaterasu/Etc to not suffer the penalty.
