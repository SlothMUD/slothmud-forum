<div class="post" data-post-id="8691" data-author="kjartan" data-date="2005-09-15T18:04:13+00:00">

I think there are in fact 8 versions, which are:

1 issued with no target, message to self

2 issued with no target, message to others in room ($n is you)

3 issued with target, message to self ($N is target)

4 issued with target, message to others in room except target ($n is you, $N is target)

5 issued with target, message to target ($n is you)

6 issued with invalid target

7 issued with target of self, message to self

8 issued with target of self, message to others in room ($n is you)

Where I say "$n is you", the following substitutions are made:

   $n   your name

   $m  him/her/it as appropriate for you

   $s   his/hers/its as appropriate for you

   $e   he/she/it as appropriate for you

Where I say "$N is target" the same substitutions apply for the target, but all uppercase.  For example, here's the messages for "growl":

Grrrrrrrrrr...

$n growls angrily, like a fierce animal.

You growl at $M.

$n growls at $N.

$n growls at you!

Growl at who?

You growl at yourself.

$n growls at $mself.

Note that you can submit emotes but we may or may not put them in depending on whether we like them, we aren't promising anything.
