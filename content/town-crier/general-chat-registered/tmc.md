TMC voting has been reset once more, TMC rank is important to Sloth as this is where we gain most of our new players from.

Voting is therefore important and I would encourage you all as fellow players of sloth to vote on a regular basis.

TMC has however changed and now requires registration to vote, this appears to be to prevent the multiple voting that has skewed the ranking in the past. Registration is a quick and painless process and I would hope that all players could take a few minutes out to register and show their support for sloth and the admins/builders who spend a considerable amount of time making sloth a place many of us enjoy spending our idle hours.
