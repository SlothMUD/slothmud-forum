Hey Clink how do u go thru a firewall at work to mud???[/quote:3dfa7acj]

Set up a wireless access point on an internet connection on another floor.  It's unlikely that every company in your building blocks outbound telnet.  Find a person willing to do this for you by going out and standing at the smoke break locations for a few hours a day until you find someone who looks appropriate to ask.

Then just hook up your laptop and you are good to go...until someone starts to track down the source of the wireless signal, but if your buddy puts it on a powerstrip with his PC, it'll only be on during business hours and he can shut it off if people start asking questions.

Note:  This is merely a hypothetical way of getting around a firewall.  I don't condone or imply that anyone should mud from work or try to avoid any firewall software.
