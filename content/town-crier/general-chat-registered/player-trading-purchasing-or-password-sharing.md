I really don't think this is necessary because its about as obvious as it gets but...

The Immortals will not get involved in any deals made involving trading/purchasing or sharing of characters.

Once your password is given to another player, whatever happens falls on YOUR shoulders, period. Do not ask us, beg us, or expect us to get involved. We will not. Its your decision and you will have to live with it. 

That also means that whatever name, wrap sheet,  reputation, etc that you traded for or bought is now yours.

Enjoy!

Splork

Addendum by Breeze

----------------------------------------------------------

If you do decide to trade/loan/buy/sell characters, figure out all the details.  What about Vaults? Bank? Auctions? Personal EQ? ..et al..  We will not be moving vault data/bank/auction lots/personal eq from character to character.  

All trades/loans/purchase/sales are at your own risk.
