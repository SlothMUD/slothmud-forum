I'm thinking of creating a Druid character and I debating between Druid/Mage/Cleric/Monk and Druid/Mage/Cleric/Necro.  Can anyone offer advice as to the strengths and weaknesses of these two classos.  Will there be a significant difference in Mana and Mana Regen?[/quote:3g4moccw]

Mana regen? No difference.  Mana total? Decent difference.  Monk gets no mana for quad and Necro gets a couple points a level.

Monk has a really nice skill list and, of course, free strikes and nice barehanded damage at levels 20+.  Necro has undeads that give you extra attacks, damage splitting options and lots of other cool stuff, but is high lag and relatively weaker undeads in quad position.

Depending how you like to play, you could go either way with it.
