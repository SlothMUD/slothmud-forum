Greetings from Moscow, it is snowing here

I was thinking to write this post or not, well i'm currently writing it.

I have to inform you all that Taron passed away in real life on Sunday, March 03, 2019. 

It was a sudden death that nobody could have ever expected.

At first i thought it was some cruel joke, unfortunately his father confirmed it.

Funeral is taking place on Wednesday, March 06, 2019.

His real name was Yuri Filimonov, he lived in Novosibirsk, Russia, he was only 37. 

And he always wanted to be perfect in everything he has ever done, that was his nature.

When he has made a decision he hardly, maybe never changed his mind.

He was my friend.

I can't really accept this fact still as we were in contact for last 20 years bleh. 

Funny that he was first player I met on my very first day in Slothmud, it was fall of '97. 

He was playing char Tr at that time, he was lvl 20 already and could solo a madman in Asylum (ofcourse he showed off) and that was impressive! That day we had a bh castle group, it was quite different from today.It was huge fun and I was totally captivated by Sloth :)

He was one of the best players in every aspect of game you can think of, great leader, true muchkin at his best, hardore player.

Some of you remember him as Jovis and many other names.

To think about it, we've spent so much time together, damn it. 

Rest in peace, buddy and cya on the other side.

Sind
