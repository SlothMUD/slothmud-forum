Let's use this topic to make suggestions about adding new epics or turning existing mobiles into epics.

Maybe a builder would eventually make some of our suggestions real.

Here's my list by continents.

**Niebelung:**

1. Ivan Von Zelkham (Ravenloft). This mobile was clearly influenced by DnD Strahd Von Zarovich. I am pretty Ivan is more than worthy to be turned into epic. After all, the game has an epic werewolf(Lupine) and an epic death knight(Rose Knight). Why not have an epic vampire too?

2. Demogorgon (DemonWeb). This is another famous DnD character, that is even seen in some other crpgs. I am pretty sure it is worthy of epic status too. Moreover killing it already grants some honor. So why not turn it into full-featured epic?

Btw, another DemonWeb mobile - whose epicization could be considered - is Lolth. Yes, I know that DemonWeb is full of epics already. But who says it's bad thing?

**Thule:**

1. Diablo (Dark Dreams). This character doesn't need no introduction. I am pretty sure it was meant to be an epic when the area was made. Unfortunately there wasn't even epic/honor system back then. He clearly deserves being epic. Just don't give it dispel fly proc...

2. Elder Titan (Titan's Isle). He just stands lonely in the area that noone ever visits. Yes, there's a titan epic(Atlas) already. But who says two epic titans can't coexist?

**Island:**

1. Diplodocus (Saurian Frontier). This mobile is probably number one in the list of mobiles whose reward doesnt worth the effort. He is already an epic in some sense, since he has tons of hps and unique proc. But the reward is nowhere near the epic level(like 250m xp + cp for the forge noone needs). Making it real epic would right that wrong.

2. Cthulhu (Valley of R'Lyeh). This is suggestion to add a new mobile. Yes, I know there's already Cthulhu in the Negative Energy Plane(AH). But that Cthulhu has nothing to do with the book the character originated from. So I suggest adding a new real Cthulhu. It would perfectly fit in the Valley of R'Lyeh.
