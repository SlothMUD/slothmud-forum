The time draws near for the first installment of the Epic Starter Package.

Some rumors have been spreading, and there has been some gossip along the grapevine about epic mobs.  I'd like to take a moment to clear up what is going to be happening over the next 2 weeks leading up to a minor quest and finally the incorporation of a new system on sloth that will provide you with an open-ended, story-driven system through which to progress beyond your current class system.

The Epic Army System (as I call it) is an idea that was born from the Celestial Quest I ran over the summer.  I also discussed the idea on gossip a couple of months ago to feel out interest.  

Basically this system is a RANK BASED system through which you will be able gain ranks (which I will not disclose at this point) by gaining Honor.

Honor will be gained through a number of different methods. 

1- through newly designed autoquests, refered to as Honorquests (HQs).

2- by killing Epic Mobs, which are mobs that are of an increased difficulty level beyound almost anything currently in the game.  These epic mobs will be of the same level of difficulty as mobs you would normally find during an immortal ran quest.  These mobs however will be permanently ingame (in various ways).

3- A new system called an Event System.  Events are mini-quest occurrences that will take place based on specific requirements, and will be mud generated (without any immortal intervention).  The events will play out in a predetermined time period, after which time they will end.  As of this starter package, you will not be required to partake in Events when they happen.  Later, once the story evolves, Events may become difficult to avoid.  The mobs in these Events have predetermined goals and seek to achieve those goals through whatever means neccessary.

This system is designed to allow for growth and evolution based on you (the players) actions and desire.  There is a main plot through which all stories will run, but the actual stories involved within that plot are written as you progress through the storyline.  The story itself though, has no real ending.  

This system is also designed to make it easier for enterprising immortals to be able to run quests at any time with mobs that are already designed to be quest-level mobs that exist in a plot line already.  

Some of the questions I have fielded about this system already are:

Is it soloable?  

ANSWER: Not really.  There may be some instances where the system can be soloed (legitmately).  But the system overall is geared to be a grouping system.  

What will happen to old autoquests and drachma system?

ANSWER: Nothing.  This epic system has been fully incorporated into the current autoquest and drachma system.  Drachma quests however have become easy and plentiful.  Drachma quests are also 1 and out setups, meaning most are not replayable.  The Epic System is "mostly" repeatable, but as with autoquests, Honorquests will not be repeatable due to the nature of the quests.  And NO, old autoquests will not give you Honor.

The starter package will include:

An array of mobs from which the stories will evolve and grow with more to be added later.

A sampling of honorquests and events, also more to be added later.

The entire system will open up a new array of concepts for you to explore and learn, all based within the currently existing world of Sloth.  

This is all I'll say for now, but the time is growing nearer.   I plan on installing the starter package on November 4th, in mid-afternoon.  No definite times have been scheduled yet.  

Keep tuned here for more information over the next couple of weeks.

------

EVENTS--- 

As aforementioned, Events will be mini quests generated by the mud. As of now (with everyone low ranked) these events are voluntary, with no penalties for not participating. As you gain in rank however, and complete objectives along the way, some events will come to you, whether you want them to or not. Mobs will seek revenge, you will be asked to do things for mobs, etc. As per some discussions about afkedness, you will be safe from these events if you are afk in a SAFEROOM or in a VAULT. Out in the open you are fair game. 

HONORQUESTS--- 

In its simplest form this are autoquests. BUT, some will be far more complex and complicated than regular autoquests. Anyone who has EVER done one of my autoquests knows that I sometimes go off on tangents from the main quest and have little subquests to prevent bottlenecks and boredom. These will be no different. My advice to ALL of you who don't do it already: 

1: Keep a log of who said what and what they said to do. 

2: Dont assume 1 step and its over, or even 3 steps and its over -READ the questers comments. 

3: Think in a logical progression if all else fails. I'm a logical person, and sometimes I will leave things out (accidentally, or on purpose) believing that logical progression will carry you forward. This applies to notetaking. If mob X tells you to go do something then come back, and 10 steps later you finally get a chance to do it, then logically, you would go back to mob X and see what happens (w/o someone needing to tell you "oh, now you can go back to X and tell him you did it). 

4: Honorquests will be hidden in many things, to be found in a variety of ways. Not all will be go ask X about this-that. But everything can be found in something that is ingame and ready to go (not on Google).

This system is as much for me as it is for you. As a builder, I LOVE testing you with tricks, traps, and puzzles. I will be building things in this system to do just that. The mobs wont all be HIT HARD with LOTS OF HP. I know that gets boring. These mobs will require strategy, thinking, in some cases planning, and in some cases even a bit of luck. But most of all they will probably require that you actually be in control of your character. Some are designed to be a terror to botters.

I have tested everything in this system as thoroughly as I can with keeping as much as possible secret. But I'm sure there will be bugs I missed. Page 8-6. Seed of Njor

-------------------------------------------------------------------------

Following The Cleansing, the Seed of Njor vanished from the mortal realm.

Many heroes have sought the ancient artifact, but none have stumbled upon

its where-abouts.  Recently reports have been flooding that King Luicus 

Dergance II may have actually stumbled upon documents written by Fathmori

that suggest that the Seed was split into two pieces after The Cleansing.

The Creators attempted to destroy the cursed relic during The Cleansing,

but ther combined power was not enough to undo the magic of Pron and

WoWiWe.  In an effort to ensure that the Seed would never be found, they

split the artifact into its two distinct parts.  One part cursed by Pron,

the other blessed by WoWiWe, both parts possessing attributes from one

another, neither entirely good nor evil.  The Creators knew that although 

they could not completely destroy the Seed, only as a single, joined 

object would itbe of any use to the mortals who so strongly sought after

it.  In the process of splitting it, the Creators summoned two creatures

to act as guardians that Pron and WoWiWe had created to protect the Seed

in just such an event.

Being of liquid form and favoring nature and the elements, WoWiWe created
