To my dismay, there was no way to connect to slothMUD via our website for quite some time. This was due to a typo in the ip address and the fact that windows had stopped using telnet. This is now fixed. 

Jamochamud, a java client, has been installed and is there for new players to try our game at the PLAY link in the middle section of our front page on the website. Hopefully this will help give our new players a chance to learn the game through something besides telnet.

I would like to thank Jeff Robinson, the creator of this client, for his help in installing and configuring it to work with our website.

Enjoy,

Splork

We will also be revealing a flash client, so that new players can choose which one to use in their attempt to play our wonderful game.
