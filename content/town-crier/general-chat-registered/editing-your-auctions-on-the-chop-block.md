<div class="post" data-post-id="10589" data-author="Weasel" data-date="2006-02-06T19:49:42+00:00" data-subject="">

Since the site moved over to the new host, there's a bug which won't allow you to change or close any auction on the chopping block. In the past you could close it and enter a new (replacement) auction when there's an input error such as was the case with Juggleblood there. Additionally you could change the closing time of the auction and presumably the minimum price. Clink is aware of the issue, although with so many new things going on ingame there are priorities before this gets fixed.. just be aware.
