Due to the response by some of you regarding some of the mobs in the

Temple of Neptune, I have taken it upon myself to update the mobs in that

area. You will find that the mobs in there aren't quite as nasty as they once

were, and I invite those of you who wish to give it a go to do so. Mainly, the

damage and hitrolls have been adjusted, and many of the hitpoint totals have

been altered as well. Overall, the area should be a bit more manageable.

Should you run into any problems that require attention, please don't hesitate

to let me know by emailing me at neptune@slothmud.org . 

Enjoy.
