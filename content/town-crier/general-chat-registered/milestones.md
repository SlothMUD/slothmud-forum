Milestone rewards will work like the following;

1) First person to reach each milestone(level 40, 2x40, etc) will receive a quest item.

2) Second person to each milestone will receive either 20 rolls from me or  drachma.

3) Third person reaching each milestone will receive  drachma.

If a player has already one a milestone eq item, they can not win a second item. We want eight different players to win eq. This person will get the second place reward in such a case.

Also, the drachma reward increases with each milestone, meaning level 40 prime is 25 drachma for second place,  and the 2x40 drachma reward is 35.

Enjoy,

Splork
