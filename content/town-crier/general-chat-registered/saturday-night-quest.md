<div class="post" data-post-id="12619" data-author="QuestReporter" data-date="2006-05-22T06:26:07+00:00" data-subject="Saturday night quest!">

On a brilliant, sunny afternoon in Tokyo, mudders from around the world were surprised by a booming echo 'Quest starting in 15 minutes.'

A Necromaster by the name of Ditheca quickly took charge, gathering a group of adventurers to meet in meditation and await the voice of the gods.

Suddenly the earth shook and the rocks groaned in agony.  The voice of Satan rang out 'Arise, King of Darkness!  I crown thee and send thee forth to conquer!'

The gods of peace and goodness then spoke, pledging a reward to those who would destroy the newly crowned King Tarrasque and retrieve the odious crown.

Although pretenders abounded throughout the world, the group quickly zeroed in on the true king.  He was found in the depths of hellwell dancing in eternal flames, and surrounded by a legion of tarrasques.

-----

One quick CF later, we managed to fight our way in and attack the King.  It took some a few tries and a lot of dying on the part of Kronk, but finally the king was cast down and the crown retrieved.

The crown was -1.3 +damage +fire resist, but in a dazzling display of bad roleplaying, the necromaster Ditheca refrained from crowning himself King of Darkness and instead returned the crown to the gods.

To reward the sacrifice, each member of the group received 50 drachma and eqpop was turned on for an hour.

Special thanks to:

Kronk for tanking

Giselle for handling the healo

Ditheca, Brady, and Truth for finding the true king

The legion of mages who unleashed the fury of the elements on the King

and Blaster for hosting a fun quest!

I believe the 15 minutes notice is probably a deliberate decision. Experience  often shows that if you announce a quest on the crier 2 things happen:

a) If it is open you get a group of 60 people that is so strong the quest mobs have to be insanely strong for the quest to achieve anything.

b) 5X40s who play twice a year log on for their twice yearly quest. Seriously we often have quest groups that only consist of a third regular players. The fifteen minute notice has the effect of rewarding current players on the whole. I know that sucks for people in other time zones.
