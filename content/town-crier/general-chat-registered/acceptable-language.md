I'll add to that by saying that I can find nothing either in help files for Sloth or on website regarding acceptable use of language or acceptable behaviour other than the above, it may be there but I missed it, at least I looked.

Also I'd like to say up front I'm not a prude and no language you can use, no flame you reply with, no trolling you can come up with will cause me any offence, also I don't seek to preach here only to ask some questions. When I make statements this is my opinion and please take them as a question 'I think .... do you agree?'

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Now what I have noticed is there is increasing tendancy to 'accept' language in SlothIII that would be unacceptable in everyday life even entirely adult situations such as a workplace or on many of the other forums I visit.

To give the most common example of this: STFU.

This is commonly used to convey a number of meanings and in a number of ways:

[list:2pu7qb5o]STFU: Please be quiet

STFU: Your question is beneath my contempt

STFU: Hello nice to see you again

STFU: You're a newbie arn't you

STFU: Please stop spamming

etc. etc. etc.[/list:u:2pu7qb5o]

Lets not be coy about it though, STFU means as I'm sure you all know Shut The Fuck Up

Why is it that this has become acceptable here when turning round to your neighbour, collegue, doctor, tennis partner or stranger in the street and telling them to shut the fuck up is likely to cause offence?

Why is it that when someone is offended by this their offence is ignored, derided, belittled and then often repeated?

Why is it that STFU is tolerated in SlothIII and on this forum when many other forum moderaters would censor it?

The word 'fuck' is used in many myriad ways but these all generally fall into 4 categories:

1. Fuck! or Fucking Hell! 

As an exclaimation

As the mud is (see above) adult orientated I can't see there is an grounds for complaint ... yet use it in gossip etc. and I understand something bad happens to you ... 

2. I fucked your mother last night. 

As an verb, the action of sexual intercourse.

I've never seen it used this way in SlothIII so I'll leave comment on this.

3. I'm fucking tired of cycling this mob or thats a fucking big mob in the castle

As an adjective to add emphasis to an expression. 

Again as the mud is (see above) adult orientated  I can't see there is an grounds for complaint ... yet use it in gossip etc. and I understand something bad happens to you ... 

4. Fuck off, shut the fuck up (STFU), you fuck, you fucking tosser.

These are I think different both here and in real life, mainly for the fact that although the same word is being used, it is directed at an individual. In my experience there are situations when the word is not acceptable, when the word is fully acceptable and this middle ground that covers a lot of life where the word is acceptable unless directed at a person present / known to those present.

It is this last use of fuck that I see as being the potential cause of difficulty in Sloth, the intention behind the use may be innocent, but the way it can be taken however can quite genuinely taken can cause understandable offence.

[list:2pu7qb5o]Is this all the fault of the person offended?

Should they shut the fuck up and ignor it / grow a thicker skin / script a word replacement?

Is there any value is pointing out to the person who caused the offence what they've done or is this more likely to lead to accusations of crybaby and flaming?

Should (or could) such phrases as STFU be banned?

Is this potential for causing offence understood by the people using STFU?

Is this perhaps why they use use it?

Should we even be discussing this? or is it a non-issue to everyone in SlothIII?

Do the Imms here have a view?

Is there a different etiquette / rule for use of language in different channels?

Do minors play SlothIII? and if they do does this make a difference? (I think my language was at its worst when I was 13)

Should there be a rule / policy to cover this area?

Has anyone actually read this far?

If someone replys with STFU does that mean they haven't read this far or just a lack of imagination?

[/list:u:2pu7qb5o]
