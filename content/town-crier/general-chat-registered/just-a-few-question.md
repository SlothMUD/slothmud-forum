I think this is considered "imm info" and the formula for stab damage was re-worked some months before the new sloth, but here is how it's been explained to me some time ago. I do not know if this is correct.

+Dam adds to stab multiplier, +Stab dam is added on after your stab.

So lets say your stab does 20hp of damage and your at +5 dam...

5 x 20 = 100hp damage.

Lets say your stab does 20hp of damage and your at +3 dam and +5 Stab Dam...

(3 x 20) + 5 = 65hp damage.

Hence +dam > +stab dam to the point I know some "elite" thieves didn't even bother with it... if they had it... it was a bonus.

There is only one stat in sloth that potentially rivals +dam and that is mana, because indirectly mana is everything.
