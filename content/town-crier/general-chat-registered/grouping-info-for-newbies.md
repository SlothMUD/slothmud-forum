Ok i just lead a Bh group and had some difficulties with the newbies.

I aslo realise there is insufficient information in grouping for newbies, especially in big groups.

Id like to state some pointers for those newbies that want to group.

1) Ok before you enter group, you need to group self then follow the leader. Do that by typing: group <name>; then follow leader.

2) Second problem i had was poeple refollowing the leader. Once youre grouped you dont need to type fol self then fol leader again, I know some people tend to refollow the leader after they get lost or enter the portal. This happened to me when i was a newb too. The bottom line is once youve followed the leader youre set and you dont need to refollow any more.

3) I also found that newbies tend to get lost when protting is concern. Prots (protects) are improtant as they keep the group alife. You should always prot the person below you unless stated otherwise. Those without prots are advice to stay at the end of the group (in order to be last in the group type: refol and youll end up last)

4) Recalling in groups are also a big NO when you get hit or youre low on hps. Cause if you recall youll break the prot chain of the group and ppl starts dying. In such situations is best that you "sing" so the group tanker can rescue you. If the tanker does not do so, just get hit and die, dont worry cause group will raise you afterwards. 

5) Ok in a case that someone youre protting dies, please take note and immediately prot the person below you (after the dead person). 

6) Ok this point really had me. Some newbies didnt know what to do in groups. In a big group, casting Blindness, Weaken, Webs, or any other offensive spells works for the group. You can also take part in healing also. Worsts come to worst you can just "assist" the tanker and do nothing. But pay ATTENTION.

7) Healo are group healers. They take turn to heal the tanker so he is a life. There is also a thing call FLOAT. People who float are healo that just heal the tanker whenever is necessery ( ie tanker hp is low). Newbies can take part in in healing by floating. 

Thats about all i have to say when group. And Impz adds that you should carry 5 recall scrolls with you all the time and red and white staff (recall staff) is important for those who are grouping.

8) Ohh and yeah, PLEASE PAY ATTENTION WHEN GROUPING!!!!! dont bot like some ppl i know, (well you can bot if youre good at it) 

Thanks.  If i left out anything, please point it out to the other thanks.

Some personal suggestions i would like to give.

1. The problem with many new players is that they do not buy enough of scroll of recalls and get lost in the world of sloth (which is pretty big). Hence, it is always advisable to stock 5 recalls at the very least whenever you go back to town. Scrolls of recall are extremely useful to bill yourself out of any dangerous situation. 

To use it, type recite recall <name>. For example, if you want to recall rith, you type recite recall rith.

2. A red and white staff is often advisable for big groups (i consider 5 or more to be big). A red and white staff is a mass recall staff that is held low and [b:10nurv79]MUST BE HELD LOW[/b:10nurv79] before it can be used. It is used to recall all your followers in your group lest yourself. 

Hence, a alias to remove your held low, get red-white-staff from container, hold red-white-staff, use staff, then recite recall self is particularly important. It is often easier to get raises in town than a area where no one might know.

3. Rith emphasised the need to pay attention. That is very important.

"Ohh and yeah, PLEASE PAY ATTENTION WHEN GROUPING!!!!! dont bot like some ppl i know, (well you can bot if youre good at it) "

However, botting is strictly not allowed in sloth. It is often just used as a joke at certain people (*poke me*) but in the real sense, no botting is allowed in sloth.

4. A good thing to note in a group as well is that if you have no protect skill (it's a warrior level 1 skill and all other non-caster classes should have it too.), please inform the leader immediately when you join the group. The leader is often oblivious due to the many things he has to do to manage a big group so giving him a grouptell and a private tell telling him/her you have no protects is a good habit.

That's all from me today. I probably add more when i think of more possible ethics.

Well, my 2 cents. I agree with all the rules that were mentioned already. But...

Here are some hints.

Unless someone has a spare beacon and will set it for you.

6. Sometimes leader says some strange words about "wild magic" rooms. These are rooms where spells you cast are screwed. That may cause your restore spell happen to be a lump coal in your hand. That's why don't cast silly spells like infravision or create food in such rooms.

7. Protects. Don't whine smth like "I have more hps than him, I have more ac, why am I below him?". Leaders usually don't care. They might put a big avatar below you and you should sit quiet and protect him.
