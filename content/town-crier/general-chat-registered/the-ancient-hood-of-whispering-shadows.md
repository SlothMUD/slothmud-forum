I think for the majority of players (non-prime thieves), the proc part of this item will prove to have limited use.

Without circle stab or garrot, non-thief primes really need to stab epic sized mobs to level it quickly. Currently you have to backstab 100 erinye sentinels, to get one proc AND they are level 41! Then if you actually use the proc on an erinye sentinel - it's completely overkill. Which means this item is geared for stabbers of group mobs. The other artifacts - at least level with spells players can use multiple times in combat. 

The item's niche use, is a prime thief who groups. That's not to say you can't "cheese" the leveling process solo... just to say that it really has no great use for non-primes, and build's procs unbearably slow doing small solo xp of which are a waste to use on small mobs.

Putting the proc to one side for a moment, the stats of the item are nice. I love the -1 spellsave, stabdam, damreduct and move regen... less excited about the MAX_DEX - that's actually a pain in my case.

I think in general artifacts could be improved better for solo. In general I have never really been a fan of "Proc Sloth" but in my opinion it would be better if procs did less damage, but more frequently, rather than atom bombs every blue moon.

Group power is already insanely OP. I've seen a 1 run Lucifer without choppers, by 6 players. If any of you remember the pre-proc days... this should be a "what the hell!"  :shock:  :shock:  :shock:  :shock: moment! The old imms will be turning in their graves. You can't have as much fulfillment without an equal amount of struggle and achievement. The game has become very high on grind, and very low on art. But these are just my opinions.

I forgot to mention that the item has problems with the "peer" command on some mobs, and unfortunately the damage the proc does is not shown in counters.
