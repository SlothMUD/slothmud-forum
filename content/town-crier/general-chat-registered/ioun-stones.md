<div class="post" data-post-id="9330" data-author="blackmore" data-date="2005-11-02T03:12:54+00:00">

You only need the stones in your inv when you mem.  Once you've memmed the spells, they can go back in your robe.  Most of us have a script similar to:

remove robe

get all.magefour robe

mem spell1

mem spell1

mem spell1

mem spell2

mem spell2

mem spell2

put all.magefour robe

wear robe

It takes a very short time for the script to run.

</div>
