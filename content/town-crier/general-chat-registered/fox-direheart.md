For future reference this is how Fox should be taken.... 

Soloing Fox would be possible, he is only a regular group mob (75m), but it's likely not to work out very well. He kills you shifted ferret, and he has a bunch of procs which make it likely that you'll just die instantly. He has a bunch of shields which make magic not a good option against him. If area resets you won't have a key to get your corpse.

First of all those who need him should first pop their OWN KEY to him from the 3 mobs that load. Alocer is the easiest to kill.

On a chop day, those with keys should form a group. Kill felicia, alocer, then unlock down to Direheart.

Tanks leads attack with demon bind, if he misses, someone else will need to get it. Best to arms him, he's likely to reflect magical spells. Best to have lambs for people he might proc during fight. If he drags someone off, leader needs to go west, and tank needs to engage. Best to do it with choppers. 5 men is a comfortable size to take him, if players are prepared.
