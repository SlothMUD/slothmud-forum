The barrel has a weight of 5 and will hold 50 for a total full weight of 55.

Not long ago I reduced the weight of rations for player convenience, thus the 1 weight.

Regarding the unrealistically low weight of shoulder guards, it's another convenience factor. I don't think there would be wide support for increasing it, but make some suggestions and we'll see what happens.
