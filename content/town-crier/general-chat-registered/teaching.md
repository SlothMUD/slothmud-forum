First of all, Kudos to the immortal staff for the introduction of this new skill.

The most difficult part of using the skill is the fact that the peristrata must be used. At times, this is all but painful to find. Especially when the peristrata happens to be in a room with an aggro mob, or a highly aggro area. With the requirement of finding shards to find the peristrata, this kind of is just as much of a pain as just cycling the spellbook twice. I'm not sure that the peristrata should be used as the only room that "teaching" should be allowed. My thought would be any reroll chamber. This would allow the use of the skill by valkers as well.

I think it would be real cool if the teacher were able to teach to a group of players and not to just one at a time. Maybe each spellbook in the teachers inventory could teach up to 3 players at a time. I can just see Shyla sitting in the Meditation Chamber teaching groups of newbies spells.

As one of the players that have been taught a spell already, I do think it is very nice addition and hope to see more of this kind of thing in the future.

Madmax
