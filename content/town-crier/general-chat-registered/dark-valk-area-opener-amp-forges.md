Dark Valk 

Dark Valk can be reached by running to Breeze's altar in the temple of Wind and typing: jump altar

BH recall run : .e3n5e3nenes2enu5n;jump altar

Dark Valk has areas and mobs from Valk (possibly excluding the Newbie College) but north/south and east/west navigation is inversed.

The mobs are evil, more powerful, and when attacked will turn into infernal forms that seem generally aggro. If one mob transforms it can initiate a chain reaction with other mobs in the room so be careful! You probably want to take a friend or two!

Shards of darkness are required for the forges and pop randomly. 

Some of areas have timed quests with sceptre - if you complete area mobs in time limit one of the EU epics spawns (Mammon for Dark Mandelbrot Mansion for example)
