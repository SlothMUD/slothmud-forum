<div class="post" data-post-id="7235" data-author="kjartan" data-date="2005-06-15T12:49:11+00:00">

Recent crashes have been new bugs that appeared during building.  (Not bugs in new code, just bugs we never noticed before).  We aren't going to do our building on a test mud, it's too much of a pain.  That would mean when anybody was building, they wouldn't be online to watch for problems or socialize.  We want to make things as pleasant for our builders as possible, and part of that is not imposing a bunch of restrictions on them even if it does mean we get the occasional freeze or crash.

We do of course test new code offline before it goes in; usually when there is a problem with new things it's because we didn't think of something, not because we didn't test it.

Regarding shutting down the mud, interestingly that's what happened in Sloth I: at one point we reduced backstab multipliers a tad, and there was so much bitching about it on the board at the west gate (no crier in those days) that we just decided it was not worth the grief and closed it.
