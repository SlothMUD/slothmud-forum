Greetings.

Earlier today I made an informal announcement regarding the use by a player

to transfer equipment between said player's characters in the inn. After some

further discussion, I'd like to take this time to retract that statement.

Going forward, we will do our best to adhere to the rules regarding Multiplaying

as outlined in the ingame help files. See 'help rules' and 'help rule 1' for more

information regarding these rules. However, again be advised of the followng:

A player may NOT transfer equipment between characters that are both

in the game at the same time. Acceptable ways to transfer equipment

between characters include (but are not limited to) the following:

1\. Using the ingame postal system. You may send equipment from one

character to another via the postal shipping method. Simply visit the

postman and ship said items to said second character, logout, login the

second character, and retrieve your items.

2\. Cooperative efforts with other players. If you choose to do so, (and

you trust another character to be helpful), you may hand over your

equipment to another player at which time you then logout your first

character, login your second character, and retrieve said item(s) from that

character you have chosen to help you transfer equipment.

Thanks for your time, and enjoy the game.

\-Neptune
