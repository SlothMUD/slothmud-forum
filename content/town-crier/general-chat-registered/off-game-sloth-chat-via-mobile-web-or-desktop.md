Greetings, slothers.

In my opinion, we could use a way to stay in touch off-game, so we could discuss things and keep people in the loop about incoming quests, eq groups or other stuff. So I created a Telegram channel, [http://telegram.me/slothmud](http://telegram.me/slothmud).

How to join:

- download mobile apps for [iPhone/iPad](https://telegram.org/dl/ios) or [Android](https://telegram.org/dl/android)

- just open a browser tab with [https://telegram.org/dl/webogram](https://telegram.org/dl/webogram)

- download [a desktop version](https://desktop.telegram.org/) for [Windows](https://tdesktop.com/win), [OS X](https://tdesktop.com/mac) or Linux or portable versions

- search for slothmud.

If you need to register, you'll need to enter your mobile phone to confirm the registration. Only your contacts will see your number, others will only see your name.

If you don't use Telegram for anything else (ie., a free-range American), then you use your use your real name and a char name in brackets, like John Smith (Neo).

When you jon, please introduce yourself: name the char(s) that you play.

If admins want to join and make quest and other announcements, I'll grant them admin rights as well.

Breeze, feel free to post a link to the channel on the homepage and a link to this thread, if you want more people to join it.
