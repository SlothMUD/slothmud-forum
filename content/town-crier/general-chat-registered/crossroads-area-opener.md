Decider wanted everyone on the island.  Minstrel (evil) and Shyla (good) were team captains.  We picked teams and had to find our respective mob in Crossroads.  Crossroads begins somewhere near Kozakura.  I believe Annie found the entrance for the good team.  Good mob was Xei, the lord of light.  Evil mob was  Ei, the lord of darkness.  After finding Ei he gave us an evil letter describing 5 mobs that contained emblems of light.  We were to retrieve the emblems and bring them back.

evil letter:

The first is a heavenly being that is pure and just. A keen reader of 

books, he is often piled up in his stacks of books to research on issues of 

justice.  ---archangel gabriel heaven

The second is in an area where energy raises and falls. He stands noble 

even with his aged eyes. Wielding a sling and a gleaming two-handed sword, he seeks to defend his home with pride.  -- seraph pos planes

The third is a race that has been good, but seen as evil to outside eyes 

that guards the emblem. A distinguished protector holds the key to break 

the seal of darkness.  --  protector irda

The fourth is an old man like no other. He fathers his followers with 

love and hope. Sitting carefully with a warrior's grace, he seeks to stamp 

out evil in this castle.  -- father castle perserverance

The fifth is locked in a cell in a mansion of his name. He is crazed 

but still pure in heart. Wishing for his lady once in a while, he seeks 

the day of light to reach.  master dalina  dalina mansion

We quickly destroyed these mobs with a few tarrasque kings in our way.  Using reign of confusion I could quickly escape these evil kings.  We collected our emblems and went back to Ei where we met another king who handled us with a restore proc.  We regened up and went back giving our emblems to Ei, he cast a spell and released the judge of Jigoku.  He was beefed up and ethereal.  We killed him and went back to the decider as requested.

  Minstrel and Shyla were voted MVP.  Minstrel got steel gladiator boots, .5 10hp.  Shyla decided to pick a purloin aura book.  Scinilescent gloves were aslo available .1 1mr 7mana, deciding infernites were just as good shyla passed on taking them.  Alot of medium level eq was passed out with each team voting on who gets a pick.  Shival, Annie, Ditheca and a few others donated eq/tokens to make sure everyone got something.  25 drachma was also awarded to each team member.  Overall this was a very well thought out quest.  I would have like to explore the area more during the quest but ths sheer mayhem kept me from being able to see much.  More carnage would have been good as well.  

  Special thanks to my team and especially Shival, Vanyel, Zukt, Ken, Tinkles who deciphered the clues pretty quickly and helped us finish early enough to kill the judge before the good group arrived.  I would also like to give a shout out to Asyral for spending alot of time planning this quest.  Congrats Asryal on Demigod!! You deserve it.   Great job leading the good team Shyla, hope you knew evil would prevail in the end.  Finishing the task first secured us the first pick of eq, with the next piece rotating to the other group.  

Mike

PS Thank Asryal next time you see him.   :twisted:
