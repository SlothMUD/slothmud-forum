I just couldn't take it anymore! I knew all the hard work would pay off one

day, and I can happily say that the day has finally come. We are now open

for business! Yes, indeed, I have come today to introduce the grand opening

of The Raven's Roost Inn. Conveniently located just a few miles from the

shoreline, our fine establishment offers several things to help those weary

travelers find rest and relaxation during those long Thalassian days and nights.

Come in and take a load off! Grab yourself a pint of our finest Thalassian ale

and catch a game with the local adventurers. While you're at it, don't forget

to try some of our fine dishes prepared by me, Kveldulfr! Then make yourself

comfortable in one of our plush suites where you'll find only the best rooms 

and accommodations. But if you're not quite ready to bed down for the

night, that's ok! There's more! The Raven's Roost Inn offers a range of

sights including an extensive library to pique the interests of the studious

types, a parlor for visiting with the other guests, a large conservatory for

viewing some of the lush Thalassian fauna, and a game room for the socially

competitive types!

Whatever your fancy, we've got it all!

Thanks, and have a pleasant stay! 

The light's always on!

------------------------------

Kveldulfr, Owner and Operator

The Raven's Roost Inn
