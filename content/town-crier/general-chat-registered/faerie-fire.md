I'm interested, does anyone use this? it ought to increase your +hit in relation to a mob its cast on ... or possibly reduce their ac ... not sure how its coded. It sounds like it ought to be as useful a debuff as curse web or blind but I rarely see it used.

"FAERIE FIRE"

Usage:         cast 'faerie fire' <target>

Cost:          5 mana

Level:         Cleric 7, Mage 2, Bard 10

Duration:      24 Hours

Accumulative:  Yes

Save Versus:   Spells to avoid completely

Damage Type:   -

Stratum:       Summoning 

Spellbook:     Faintly glowing

Prerequisite:  -

See Also:      -

In spite of its name, this spell is composed entirely of threads of air.

It creates a thin current of glowing air about half an inch from the target's

skin, which makes said target much easier to see and therefore easier to hit.

Further castings will make the glowing mesh stronger and more visible.

This spell can be cast upon the caster itself to impressive effect,

and it is quite easy to master.  It's used as part of religious and civilian

festivals in many cultures.
