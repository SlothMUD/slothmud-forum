Another Blaster greed quest:

After the beating of Satan's number one helper, the God of Fire and Lava, a powerful force was summoned by all the evil of the world.

This force hunted down and found the perfect immortal body to posses leaving any good within that immortal body behind.

This now evil immortal is living secretly upon all other immortals high up in the heavens and is watch down on all of his possible mortal targets.

He was last seen standing upon the foot of a great mountain peering around for mortals and planing his destructive tactics.

The evil immortal has promised to bring pain, suffering, and hatred to all the mortals of the world in an effort to strengthen Satan's powers.

With the aid of his deadly angels who are summoned at will, he will slowly and painfully terminate all mortals who approach him.

If you dare, seek him out and attempt to destroy him, but beware, he is like all other immortals, almost unbeatable.

If you choose to allow him to roam the world, sooner or later you will become a target of his.

Even Jake, Splork and Blaster had trouble with this evil force, and have now asked for your help in making sure his life is terminated.

He has now taken possesion of one of the most deadly and powerful axes known to any immortal. 

If you manage to obtain this axe, you may choose to keep it, but no one but yourself will be rewarded.

If you succeed, and turn in the axe, the rewards will be well deserved, but if you fail, you will be lucky to walk away with only your life and perso
