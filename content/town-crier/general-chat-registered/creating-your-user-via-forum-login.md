We have removed the ability to login to the website CMS due to security concerns.

To retrieve your user's password:

1\) browse to login button

2\) click resend activation email

3\) enter username

4\) enter email address

At this point, you should receive your reactivation for this forum.

If you do not remember your email that was used with your account, you may email benq@slothmud.org or splork@slothmud.org and we can set your account for you. However be prepared to prove the username is yours.

Also, there has been some reports of links not working, maps missing, etc. It was impossible for us to test all possible scenarios on the new site so please start a bug report thread and start posting those that you find. Please include the names of the missing maps, the link address that is not working, etc. Please include your browser if it is a browser specific issue. We tested most internal pages with IE and Firefox.

Splork
