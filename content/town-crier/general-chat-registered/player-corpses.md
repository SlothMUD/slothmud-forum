<div class="post" data-post-id="32810" data-author="Stark" data-date="2011-08-08T21:58:01+00:00" data-subject="Player corpses">

Since my last comments on the "talk of exp and 8x40" topic, I have since started a new character (Timber), and have been enjoying the game again.  The increased xp gain is being enjoyed.  :)  My thanks again to all the immorts that make this game possible that I can keep coming back to.  

I had made an off topic mention of this topic and I wanted to visit it in its own thread.  Currently, having a corpse full of equipment laying about the world is the most dangerous situation you can find yourself in, because the moment your eq isn't on your living person, it's at a serious risk of loss.  Server crash?  You're out of luck till you can petition an admin.  Your internet connection died?  Ouch!  Who knows what will become of your stuff!  Real Life issue that forces you to leave?  Too bad, because your game will be screwed for awhile when you get back.

Everything that your character holds dear is sitting in that rotting carcass.  My proposed solution: why not make your corpse tied to your character and saved along with the equipment in it?  Let's say you died at kobold sage, but you have to log off (because of RL issues or frustration), so you rent (or void), but your corpse is saved and disappears with you while you're away.  You can now log on the next day, and your corpse will appear in the world with you and you can fight your way back to the kobold sage to reclaim your corpse, gear, and dignity at your leisure.  

There's some loose ends that would need to be tied up 

- Probably only save 1st corpse

Have notifications that you've got a corpse sitting out there and subsequent corpses won't be saved

Still pay rent penalties on gear in corpse

?

Should probably have the corpse persist in the world for a period of time after the player logs off if you don't want players to circumvent mob looting mechanics by just not logging back in till someone is sitting on top of where the corpse will appear.
