ancient temple with depictions of jaguars and winged snakes. Having 

discovered a veritable treasure of knowledge, they returned through R'lyeh

and back to the areas south of the Rainbow Valley to bring us the gift of

this discovery.

The journey is arduous, and there are a few dangers along the way. But we

feel with the right team of peopleto clear the dangers, we can once again

reclaim the lost knoweldge of the Nuahtl people and their practices. The

with Neptune's grace we feel we will be successful in our neverending search

for truth and knowledge.

There is something written upon it:

Reaching the crest, our team of men found signs of more travel including

some minor pot shards and ruined implements of the Nuahtl people. They 

soon followed the trail south and east from the crest where the entered

a thick jungle. There, covered in vines, they came upon a sheer cliff face

where one of them spotted the remnants of a structure on top of the high

precipice. With much luck, they were able to climb the vines and reach the 

top of the cliff.

There is something written upon it:

Having excavated the site of the ancient peoples known as the Nuahtl

just south of the Rainbow Valley, we have recently unearthed possible

locations of several other ruined city-states that may lie deeper within

the interior of the island. In the fallen city of Ch'khtl Na'alrath we

found tablets that were translated as trade partners to the south, and 

described trade routes into the modern areas of the Great Ones.

There is something written upon it:

We sent a team of archaeologists into the area shortly thereafter to 

take a look around, and indeed there were signs of ancient travel.

Talking to some of the native peoples in the Valley of the Great Ones, 

we were told that the area had been used for centuries by the peoples

that lived in that area of the island. One such little man with crater-

like eys told us we'd do best to reach the crest if we wanted to find

the oldest of these trade routes.
