<div class="post" data-post-id="30541" data-author="Thraxas" data-date="2009-12-24T09:31:44+00:00">

thinking about this, I wonder whether the whole concept of the jail system needs a rethink.

Jail prevents a player from playing, to my mind there are so very few situations when you want this to happen. My list of offences so severe that a player should be prevented from playing is as follows:

1. Player deliberately tries to break the game

2. erm see number 1.

Killing shopkeepers while stupid (sorry Stab) isnt an offence that should stop someone from playing.

Killing players or stealing from players again should be punished but not by stopping someone playing .... if it gets to that point (i.e. repeat offenders) for these offences I think it should be a consious decision of an imm not an automatic game event.

I guess what I'm trying to say is if you're going to lock us up, give us a way to break out or be broken out i.e. a different aspect of playing the game rather than a way a stopping us play.

If punishment is still needed beyond that then someone other than a script should make that decision.
