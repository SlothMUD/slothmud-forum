I'd like to say a few things in this regards since EQ is my fav thang'-

Over the course of say the first 10 years that I have played here, items were traditionally introduced in its category or say bracket (dam or hit for example) would follow en suite behind it's predecessor. The new item could possibly be an upgrade and if not the other way. This would then compensate towards a forged item. 

Over next couple of years, general EQ took a new curve because drachma Gear(Quest Gear) got introduced. These pieces were deemed optimum despite your build and taste. With the birth of Drachma Gear - EQ ceiling was cemented and no generic or forged gear brought in could breach these said brackets.

Moving forward a couple more years, caps were raised not once but twice. Your stats that affect the way you gear and play almost saw a 50% top up. Gear or items previously contributing to these brackets had them broken/scaled down into gems making even prized forged gear look obsolete. Indirectly, what happens to your popular drachma gear?
