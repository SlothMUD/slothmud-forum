I agree animals having coins is stupid, and so are easy island mobs that are loaded. But how can you fill this void? 

You could create markets for animals parts... furs, bones, scalps. 

Group mobs could load treasure items that could be coded to give a set amount to each player in the group... ie, a group of 5 gets 10k each, instead of 10k split 5 ways.

Item values could be fixed to be realistic. Take for example the mystic platemail, that was such a good item even avatars wore it, yet it was easy to come by and sold in the shops for nothing. Yet something like Infernite Gauntlets prob had a shop value of 1m or less.

Item values could be calculated by a formula. Something like MobSize * Poprate * ItemStats * ItemLevel. If your going to nerf coins selling items to shops should become a viable enterprise... not just mage stones, tokens and hellwell pops.

You could create markets for resources... along the lines of the gem stones that pop in poisedon. They could also be found in the various parts of sloth located underground... you could use a terrain flag.

I know auctions are based on supply and demand, but there were aspects of the old sloth that compounded the problem. When I ran eq for coins I could make 10-20mill a day if I could cycle different mobs for multiple customers... but I soon found some mobs were not worth dealing with... 

For example...

Adrian (DWeb) 39 Cycles for 2 Bracelets... 40min repop... 26hrs of cycling for 2,925,000 coins.

Dwarf Wight (Moria) 3 cycles for 3 Belts... 40min repop... 2hrs of cycling for about 30,000,000 coins.

I think it would be good if this kind of thing could be corrected. You can make good coins popping eq. I made 200mill in a few weeks to buy my clan a vault, but towards the end I was under no option but to choose my customers and mobs based on repop times, values and cycles. If a formula was used to create item values, things would be a lot more consistant, running other areas would become just as good for coin options.
