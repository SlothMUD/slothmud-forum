I forgot to post yesterday but the reboot brought us some bugfixes (discussed in other posts) and a new command.

> HELP LOOT COMMAND

LOOT "LOOT COMMAND"

See Also:     GET DROP PUT

The LOOT command is similar to performing a "GET ALL" from a container except

that it only looks at containers that are on the ground.  If you are the

peculiar type of person who frequently travels the land of Jord whilst carrying

the recently deceased, you may find the LOOT command useful as it will skip

over the corpses in your inventory.

The LOOT command can be entered without specifying a container.  In this case

the command will default to the first corpse on the ground.
