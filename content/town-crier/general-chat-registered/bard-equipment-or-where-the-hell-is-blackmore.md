<div class="post" data-post-id="13223" data-author="Tetsulunathaya" data-date="2006-06-30T19:31:25+00:00" data-subject="">

Bardic Instruments stats: X%/Y%/Z

X% = Duration of Song Increase. This effects how long the song will continue to take effect after you stop playing/singing. Higher the number, longer it lasts.

Y% = Reduction in cost of songs. Affects both the initial cost to start the song, as well as the amount to maintain it. 

Z = Additional Targets Affected. Just as is sounds. Raw bonus to the number of targets hit by your songs, offensive or benificial.

Bardic instruments do not stack, and when you sing it will automatically select the 'best' instrument you have equipped. I think with the play command you can select between intsruments, but haven't really tested.
