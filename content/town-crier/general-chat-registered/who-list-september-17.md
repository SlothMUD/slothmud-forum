> Apart from necro's... not many people play a prime of the new classes... and even less of those play them through to avatar...

> Why do you think that is so?

Even prime necromancers arise less often than any traditional prime class. I'd mainly credit several forms of momentum:

\- Many characters still played today predate the new classes.

\- People generally have more exposure to the traditional classes from other fantasy environments than to the new classes.

\- Lack of new-classo prime characters people want to imitate.

> <strong>jezer wrote:</strong>
> Prime Monks are bad thieves?

Prime monks are not bad absolutely, but the advantage of prime monk over non-prime monk does not rival the advantage of a prime thief over a non-prime thief.

Monks have an unfortunate fundamental stat order, Con - Dex - Str - Wis - Int, making many of them roll str fourth. Thieves other than Th/Wa roll str third. 

> <strong>jezer wrote:</strong>
> Prime Druid only good for regen?

Lack of benefits of prime druid over secondary druid.

> <strong>jezer wrote:</strong>
> Prime Bard just plan suck solo?

Prime bards solo well. They have major advantages over non-prime bards, so I can't explain their dearth on technical grounds.
