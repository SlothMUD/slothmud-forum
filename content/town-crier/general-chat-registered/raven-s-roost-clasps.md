<div class="post" data-post-id="34247" data-author="*Neptune*" data-date="2013-04-19T15:15:35+00:00" data-subject="Re: Raven&#x27;s Roost clasps">

Hey, thanks for your suggestions. We're always looking for ideas like this.

It's true that this item is part of a forge, and that is indeed it's prime function.

Also, in this case, the item can be used as a regular item without stepping on

the proverbial toes of the Clasp of Life and Death. While this wasn't actually

an intention of our's, it does seem to work out nicely in this context.

Personally, I've always been a fan of dual-purpose items.

The real problem with making a hasty changes such as putting an Undead

Control clasp on another slot is that you run the risk of creating

obsolescence in a number of pieces of equipment. For example, what good

would shield of stolen souls be if you could just apply UC to any shield? Its

value would be diminished. In an attempt to maintain some semblence of

relevance with existing equipment, I'd have to look at things a bit closer

before I could make that sort of decision. This, however, in no way removes

the possibility that something like that couldn't appear in the future on

another item in another area. Also, bear in mind that there is a large

number of items that either don't appear on the eq list, haven't been loaded

and identified yet, or both.

-Neptune
