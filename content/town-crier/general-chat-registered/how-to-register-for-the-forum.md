This post is for people trying to register on the forum, of which I frequently hear that people are unable to do. I think most times the issue is that people put the wrong skills in the wrong columns, because the phrase "Who knows what these are???" can be taken either way.

Prediction, Swiftkick, Sharpen, DeathTouch, Urban Stealth need to be in the left column. Improved Backstab, Winds of Fury, Improved KickStart, Hands of Fury, Feet of Speed need to be in the right column. I don't expect that providing the answers will open us to spammers, and if anything the question has done more harm than good by keeping true players out.
