Around 3:00 system time, the Lyrran Priestess announced the awakening of the first relic, and that her scrying had revealed it was in the southern hemisphere, south of Valkyrie.  She was unable to discern anything more on its location, but concerned adventurers went on the hunt for the celestial relics.  Yinao found that the Dark Angel in Hades had possession of the Celestial Halo of Radiance, and a large group assembled to recover it.  Despite assistance from a legion of lemures and the demon, Nalfeshnee, the angel was defeated and the relic recovered.  The adventurers were treated to this sight:

<div class="post" data-post-id="18200" data-author="Akasha" data-date="2007-04-27T06:23:04+00:00" data-subject="Celesitials minis updates - Gauntlets of the Forger">

At about 8pm system time, the Lyrran Priestess announced the arrival of the next relic, somewhere deep underground.  After much searching, Atwell and Yinao found a much souped up Balor Demon...and lost their hp.  Whatever...lives are expendable when you've got high-powered clerics on your side.  This was fortunate since the balor demon smashed the large group of adventurers several times over and made the rest flee wildly around the matterhorn with its proc.  Sadly despite the extreme effort required for its death, it popped only a heavy iron ring.

The creature's existence led Verin to believe the relic was deeper inside the matterhorn, and indeed Hyperion carried the Celestial Gauntlets of the Forger, and was guarded by legions of quasits.  The legions were easily dispatched.  The God, not so much.

After the first cf stick was used, the god was still barely scratched and most of the group was dead.  Several tries later, a plan to massively overheal the tank was implemented and no more than two characters died each run following that.  The Lyrran Priestess showed up in the middle of one run and restored the party several times.  So extreme was the exchange of hp and mana on both sides that the world was torn asunder and brought to a Crashing halt.

The gods soon restored the world to its prior condition, and the party returned and rapidly finished off the errant god, returning the Celestial Gauntlets to their home in the heavens and gaining 30 drachma.

Mortals now wait anxiously for word of the next relic's return...[/quote:1gjext1y]

The Lyrran Priestess once again informed the world of an appearing relic:

'A glimmer awakens... a place full of serpents..  evil slithers at every corner'

'It is dark, but the glimmering light of yet another relic has peaked  through the darkness'

'i hear the mumblings of a powerful mage nearby, say the relic'

Deep inside the Dark Covenant, behind bon and cheval, the Celestial Ribbons of Peace were found in the possession of Tanra, Mistress of Spiders.

A truly beautiful demoness, Tanra flexes her tempting, voluptuous body, 

which shows clearly beneath the tight black leather body suit she wears.  

She smiles a wickedly alluring smile as she motions for you to come closer.  

Her beauty is so captivating that it is easy to lose sight of the six extra 

legs folded neatly behind her, and her large spider's abdomen.  

Tanra, mistress of spiders is in excellent condition.

Tanra had a nasty habit of poisoning everyone who came to fight her, and spawned swarms of spiderlings who did damage all around, plus had some sort of lag proc.  Despite these great powers, Tanra was defeated much faster than the previous relic-holders, and the tank Kong somehow managed to only require raising once.

The ribbons were returned to the gods for yet another large chunk of drachma, and the Lyrran Priestess offered an update on the Relics' status:
