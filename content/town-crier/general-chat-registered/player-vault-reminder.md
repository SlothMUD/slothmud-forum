Lot of vault purchasing going on lately. So to answer a few questions.

[help housing] for how to buy a house.

[help house] for how to customize your house.

Don't forget to lock your house as someone could use a script to open the vault. I've found a house or two unlocked when looking for empty lots.

House doors are pickproof and sneakproof. If you notice yours is not and you would like it fixed I'm happy to do so.

Yes you can have vaults on diff continents.

No you can't choose the location of your house. If you have a preference within the housing district, that's fine. But you can't have it outside the designated housing areas.

Cheers.
