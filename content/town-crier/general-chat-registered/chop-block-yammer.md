<div class="post" data-post-id="16388" data-author="Avatar" data-date="2007-01-20T02:55:09+00:00" data-subject="Auction">

[quote:2zsgy85p]And sure, you trolls can say 'so bid 3m +10c yourself', and then the snipers will go to +15, until you have to bid a full million extra just to be sure someone won't jump your bid by just a couple coins. that's lame.[/quote:2zsgy85p]

There are no snipers.  You guys only see this bid at the last minute and see what you want to see.  If you had bid 5 mil on the item, it would have only applied the minimum amount to show you winning.  If you're winning at 3 mil and someone bids higher, they don't win.  More of your 5 mil is applied to keep you winning the item.  So now it's at 3.5 mil.  If that's still a bargain for the item, someone may post a slightly higher bid.  They still don't win, the item goes up to 4 mil and you're still the winner.  The only way you lose is if someone actually outbids your 5 mil.

These bids that you call sniping are actually bargain hunters.  If they see an item going for less than it's worth, they will bid on it.  If the item is going for a fair market value, they won't bid.

You can do the same bargain hunting.  If you see an item going for less than it's worth, post what you think its worth.  You don't have to wait until the last minutes of the auction, you could bid this way as soon as the item comes for sale.
