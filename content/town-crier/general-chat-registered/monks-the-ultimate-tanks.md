<div class="post" data-post-id="10010" data-author="Teron" data-date="2006-01-08T07:56:44+00:00">

Hello, Jezer

As a wa/mo, I think I get about +3 dam reduction naturally at 2x40.

I also have two dam reduction items, which makes it to about +5 dam reduction.

Now about the effectiveness. I do notice the difference. Since the dam reduction is, basically, a hp saved per hit (it cant be more than a hp per dam reduction, right?), you can save about 10-30hps per round of combat. This is pretty good as it is.

If anyone doubts whether dam reduction helps, get a 1x40 monk or 2x40 xx/mo to -9ac, solo a bit, then try soloing same mobs w/ the same ac on any other classo. You should notice a difference.

As 12345 noticed, one can use counter and dodge in groups, but its pretty incoherent while tanking (have to watch out not to mess w/ a whiner, and watch for the messages, etc). Becides, lately parry worked far better than counterattack (Ive only seen counterattack work once or twice during the duration, while parry used to work almost every round..now parry works worse, but is still better than typing counter every 8-10 rounds).

If you want to go defensive, sure, go for it. Everyone has to find his unique playing style and classo and stick with it.

If you do not get fun playing Jezer, you may, indeed, try creating a character with the classo you will thoroughly enjoy playing (mo/wa/cl/ma, maybe?)

That being said, I do think monks can tank, and be an efficient tank too, but only when there is no warrior around with same or better ac. (there may be exceptions too, though).
