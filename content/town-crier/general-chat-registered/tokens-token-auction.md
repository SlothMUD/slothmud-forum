If the token auction is lagging the game, then I suggest we remove it.  Since tokens are not as rare as they once were, the token auction has become a holding place where people can hoard them rent free.

I suggest in place of a token auction, we put in a slot machine where you put in a hex and 1d100 x1000 gold falls out. 

The auction isn't going to work out for tokens. It's always just going to be abused to hoard them and avoid rent, unless it is radically altered. If it's slowing down the game, please take it out.

<div class="post" data-post-id="37418" data-author="*juggleblood*" data-date="2016-10-19T21:53:15+00:00">

Well yes, I am not blind to the irony.

I thought about nerfing it, but then the dynamic that I see is that when someone new wants to roll a character, other players go to the pits and get them a bunch of tokens for free and that's not a bad thing.

But, let's say that I made tokens rarer again.  That would not make the reroll auction stop filling up, because people would continue to hoard them there at prices above what they expect it to sell for in order to save on item count and rent. We could apply a bunch of extra rules and fees and whatnot, but then I fear it'd be a lot of work for the end result of no one happy with it anymore.

That's why I'm just in favor of keeping the hexes loading in the pits like they do, but taking the auction house out and replacing it with a slot machine that doles out 1d100 x1000 gold. An alternative solution is to have both and see if the slot machine cuts down on the amount in auction block.
