<div class="post" data-post-id="39229" data-author="Gorka" data-date="2019-07-04T03:36:08+00:00" data-subject="The Smarter Nerfs To Warriors...">

For those who don't know, warcry has been nerfed for warriors solo - net effect for me is about -100hp, -3 damage and a spell save for me.

This isn't a post to cry about it, just suggest a far smarter nerf... if the intention was to simply stop warriors from retreat and warcry tactics... it was far smarter to move this benefit to oath of victory - where retreat and re-crying was not an option.

Similarly if second wind is to be changed, it should only work for skills, not spells.

I understand that warcry was being abused, nerfs are unpleasant and sometimes necessary, but I think the lighter hand, sit back and watch approach was probably better.

Scoff as some players will, my warrior tactics never involved the prolific abuse of warcry or second wind, I find the movement loss of second wind mana inefficient, and for xp I only kill mobs I can crush and have no reason to use warcry/retreat tactics. The effects to me don't appear to be that great, perhaps the loss of damage and the spellsave was most disappointing. But what is damage these days when you have proc weapons...

</div>

<div class="post" data-post-id="39231" data-author="Gorka" data-date="2019-07-04T14:45:10+00:00" data-subject="Re: The Smarter Nerfs To Warriors...">

Another smarter idea would have been to not discharge the warcry until a mob dies... an adaption of the current oath of victory code. The problem with the warrior class, is not its power, but the techniques. There was no need to nerf the power of the warcry that a warrior earns with 10,000's of group mob kills... all that was needed was to make sure a mob was actually dead before a warrior can warcry again. I understand the change, but it over reached.
