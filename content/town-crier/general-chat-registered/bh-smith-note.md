Alright, here's the gist of it.

We were getting echoes of metals clashing in the sky for a bit, and soon anvil's started falling on people. Each anvil strike would immediately kill a player.

Needless to say a quick locate object on anvil lead to the finding of the anvil sign near pipoit, near the village of sheol. THis had absolutely nothing to do with the quest and soon immorts made the BH castle smith gossip some. Curious adventurers headed that way and found a peculiar note on the smith. I believe it was Kheldar who found the aforementioned smith. Killing the smith allowed us to secure the following note :

[quote:exnu1epr]

Quit playing with your pathetic charms. I have a much more important task for you. You are to melt down and combine several

rare metals for me. Once amalgamated, pour them into special containers forged by one of your own. He will be sending them in

to smelt. I will be sending a messenger to tell you where to ship the canisters upon completion.

[/quote:exnu1epr]

My master, your master, Malign requests your next batch of alloy.
