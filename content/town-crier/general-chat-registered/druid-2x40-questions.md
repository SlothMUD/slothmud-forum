To clear up the Xeg/Xag thing:

As with all supplicants, level level requirements consider your Cleric level, and all subsequent levels in your classo, but NOT previous levels. I'm not sure what second level Xeg/Xag require - Eudaemon, IIRC, is 40/34, meaning that you need level 40 cleric and level 34 in your next class in your classo.

Further, summoning Xag's (the good ones) can be awkward, because not all corpses work for them - it's not a matter of the size of the corpse, either. For example, Shadows in the catas will work, but skels, zombies, ghouls, and wights will not.

Is it not working because you're using the wrong corpses ('...rejects these piddling sacrifices!') or because you're too low level ('unworthy of such aid/fear to summon such a dangerous creature')?
