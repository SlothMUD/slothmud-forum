I just wanted to mention that it would be useful if some of the new bard songs had different "take effect" emote functions then existing spells.

Currently I know 

**"Song of the Mist"** and **"Knights Blessing"** both give:

"You are inspired by %0's song!", 

which is the same that brothers in arms uses. They already have their own "dropping" emote, but it would be handy, for many reasons (scripting purposes, as well as just visually keeping track of spells), to have their own take effect lines .

I also notice that neither of these songs show up in the 'You are affected by" area in the Score command, where most other songs do.
