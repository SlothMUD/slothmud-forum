The first is a heavenly being that is pure and just. A keen reader of

books, he is often piled up in his stacks of books to research on issues of

justice.

The second is in an area where energy raises and falls. He stands noble

even with his aged eyes. Wielding a sling and a gleaming two-handed sword,

he seeks to defend his home with pride.

The third is a race that has been good, but seen as evil to outside eyes

that guards the emblem. A distinguished protector holds the key to break

the seal of darkness.

The fourth is an old man like no other. He fathers his followers with

love and hope. Sitting carefully with a warrior's grace, he seeks to stamp

out evil in this castle.

The fifth is locked in a cell in a mansion of his name. He is crazed

but still pure in heart. Wishing for his lady once in a while, he seeks

the day of light to reach.
