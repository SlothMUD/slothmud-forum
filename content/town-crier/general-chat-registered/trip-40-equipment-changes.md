Some items may be re-introduced or new items may be added, but this is the list that will go into affect within the next couple of days.  Your existing items, if they gets changed, will be changed automatically.

Exchanges on items will be capped at 1 mil cost until sept.1, at which point they go back to the usual exchange rates.

*) a sorceress' favor: DROP

1) dragon-skin laces:  clasp for any location, 1 dex 20 hp regen

2)a polished spectrolite charm: clasp for legs, 40 hp, 10 hp regen

4) Axe of Dwarvish Lords: slashing weapon 5d6 +5 hit +1 dex 

5) Adamantine Cuirass of the Ancients: body armor 2.7 ac +30 hp

6) Tooled Girdle of Ogre Kings: waiste .6 ac 1 wis +1 to hit

7) Arcane Tome of Power: held low .5 ac +1 dam +8 mv regen

8) Rogue's Dagger: backstab weapon 3d4 +2 str +3 dam + proc

9) Sable Ring of the Dark Lords: finger 35ma 2 mana reg .4 ac

10) The Crown of Thorns: head 1.2 ac 2 str 3 mana regen

11) Shroud of the Exalted Priest: body armor 2.4 ac 20 mana 2 mana regen

12) Mithril Sleeves of the High Paladin: arms .9ac 20 mana 12 hp regen

13) The Staff of Ra: light source held high 35ma 4 mana regen

14) Jeweled Gauntlets of the Magi: hands .4 ac 25 mana 2 con

15) Ancient Cincture of Vigor: waiste .3 ac 40 mana 2 str

16) Tower Shield of the Great Lords: shield 1.2 ac 1 dex 1 con

*) Runed Tooth of the Arch-Lich: DROP

17) Battle Spiked Armbands of the Night Prince: arms .8 ac 1 dam 1 con

18) Staff of the Magi: renamed from the elders staff, weapon 3d4 +20 mana +3 mana regen + fireball proc

19) Elven Chain Leggings of the Mystic Guard: 1.7 ac 12 spell bonus 12 heal bonus.

Most of the items are intentionally kept fairly generic, although a few will obviously appeal to certain classes/class combinations.
