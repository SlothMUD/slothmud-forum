<div class="post" data-post-id="35222" data-author="*Neptune*" data-date="2014-06-04T15:55:34+00:00">

Thinking here by 'stash' you mean 'cache' :

If there's a cache in a room, certain types of items will be prioritized to load

in the room cache rather than inventory/worn. While I won't go into detail about

what items do this, I will make a note here that if you discover a room cache when

you hit a mob once, then the room cache will be there when you hit the mob again

until the system reboots by some means. During those times, the priority

does not change, and therefore those types of things that were prioritized

to the room cache will again be priortized to the room cache as they were before.

I hope this helps.

-Neptune
