Quickly the little mud, operated by a handful of college students gained recognition across the world â€“ from the United States to Russia to Australia and points in between and beyond.  The goal in the beginning was to give people a friendly atmosphere in which to play, and a means with which to interact with friends across the globe. 

      Five years later, SlothMUDIII was born from the ashes with a new owner, and fresh ideas, and a new direction for the MUD.  Since August 13, 1997, this direction has been to make SlothMUDIII one of the best MUDs on the internet.  With hundreds of players worldwide, a friendly and robust atmosphere, and hours of enjoyment for everyone, SlothMUDIII has risen to the challenge of maintaining a free-to-play system with no donation system for elite equipment and an open-to-all mentality, all while improving game play by leaps and bounds through the years.
