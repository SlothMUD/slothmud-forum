I've yet to reach him with his international number nor could he with mine. The hotel he stayed at was pretty far off from the city and it would have been a bomb for us to take a cab down and back.(We're only college students:( ) So Lis was kind enough to make the trip up into the city. We spoke briefly through sloth and came up with a meeting point.

We never spoke on the phone nor did we keep in touch via en-route to the city. For all I know, we only came to a conclusion: where to meet and a time. Due to uncertain mishaps, time was delayed on our side. All in all, we arrived at KLCC(meeting point) an hour late. Alex(Mech) whom I just met on sloth a week prior to Lis's arrival also showed up, which was a good thing. So total was about 5 of us, now time to find us a russian! 

Lis and I had agreed to meet up nearby the water display just a few floors down from where I met the other guys. It wasn't much of a hassle until I actually got there. After a long winding search, I finally found our Lis and we went up for a late meal with the other guys.
