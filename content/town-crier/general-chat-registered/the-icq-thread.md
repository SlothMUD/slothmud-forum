It looks as though a lot of you folks don't understand how to get saved

on SlothMUD when you die.  The first, best, help is going to be a scroll

of recall.  A couple other things that will help you get your sorry ass raised

are 

1: ICQ - It's an instant messenger thing.  You can just use Trillian

and it will manage ICQ/AIM/Yahoo/MSN, you don't even have to have the

other shit installed.  When you get ICQ, put you number in your title for a

few days or so, so that people will know to add you.  Get some other

numbers too.

2: Channels - Join a channel ingame with a few smart people who know the

mud.  Hosers usually works, or the ~dragonsworn~ channel.  When you die,

break your link and log back in.  This kicks you out of the channel and a lot

of people will send you a tell to make sure you are not dead (which you

are, so it's cool).
