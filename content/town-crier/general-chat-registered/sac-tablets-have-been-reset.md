With the year coming to an end, it was time to reset the sacrifice tablet

tallies. This means that if you wish to sacrifice corpses to a god of your

choice, you just ned to start sacrificing corpses once again. We do, however,

enourage those of levels 21 and higher to choose another god so that the

Valkyre boards don't get too clogged in the long run which is why this was done. 

Also, things were getting a little long in the proverbial tooth, so to speak. 

I realize this may be a minor inconvenience for some of you, but in the end

it is for the best. Consider this a part of our standard game maintenance

that happens from time to time. 

Thanks, and happy hunting!
