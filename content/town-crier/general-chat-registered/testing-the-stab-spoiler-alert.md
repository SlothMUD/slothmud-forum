Why is this significant ? Because it takes away the randomness and allows you to see the bones of the game if you will.

I guess we all know how sloth3 stab system works as there were plenty of people who were interested in exploring and testing the games a bit further

than people do now. Anyways, it operated on a multiplier system where every damage added 17 hp to your stabs, i.e there were 17 multipliers

they got added from level 1 untill level 40 where you were maxed out.

How do you find your multipliers ? You try to stab something with 1d1 dagger and X dam, then you try to stab the same target with 1d1 dagger and X+1 dam

That way, the difference between those 2 values is your multiplier. I heard how sloth4 stab system was different from sloth3 so i was eager to test it and find out

what is what.

And so here are the results for you guys to see, if you dont like reading logs then skip to the end.
