FWIW, your xp on your artifacts are tied to your character name, so if someone else tries to feed your artifact, the artifact will simply tell you that you don't have enough xp to level. If you happened to have enough xp to do it, you could take over someone else's artifact, but having multiple artifacts would do you absolutely no good... I made sure of that. I have no issues with making them personal, though... it was a simple oversight.

Also, you can't use them in safe rooms. If you have found some situations where that isn't true, please let me know. That's part of the same code that prevents artifacts from being used in the arena.

The only time the clasps show up on the eqlist is when reimbursements are made by the gods... clasps are autoremoved from their item in the process, which I don't like, but ok whatever. If anyone has seen other circumstances in which the clasps have fallen off, please let me know and I will do whatever I can to fix it.
