There's actually a customizable menu on this site, and I use it and have done for a long time. It puts an additional menu box on the lower left hand side, and you can put whatever links in it you like, including external links. I have links directly to the chopping block, eq list, main maps page, and voting sites from there.

The way you set it up is click on the 'home' link that's on the top left menu right now, then on resulting page on the lower right you will see a box titled 'main menu'. Click on the link titled 'my account' in that box. On the resulting page, click on the link with a picture of a house above it, titled "Edit your home page".

Now just check the "Enable your personal menu block", and then write in HTML all the links you want to appear, for example:

```
<a href="http://www.slothmud.org/modules.php?op=modload&name=PNphpBB2&file=search&search_id=newposts" target="new">New Posts</a><br />

<a href="http://www.slothmud.org/eqlist/maproom.php" target="new">Maps</a><br />

<a href="http://www.slothmud.org/eqlist/index.php" target="new">EQ List</a><br />
```

Save changes, and you're done. On the left hand side of any page from now on, there will be a new block of links that are all yours. Kewl huh.
