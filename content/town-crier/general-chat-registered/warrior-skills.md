You can't flee to debuff or save someone's ass, you gotta sit there and take it like a.. tank.

wa: shield block - takes a whole round of attacks for the whole group. This skill should do around 200 to 500 damage, or perhaps just a regular attack from the mob. Good to use if protects gets screwed cuz someone dead/missing, or if tank is dying.

wa: impale - used with great spears. Acts like a deathblow, Impales mob right through its body. With impale, you could do normal damage (or close to that of a kick) but has 20% damage to do 3x damage.

wa: chop(for lack of a better name) - used with great axes. Acts like a deathblow. With chop, you could do normal damage (or close to that of a kick) but has 25% chance to stun the mob or do 3x damage.

Just remember, you can only riposte with a sword, so swords would still be used for the tank.

wa: leap - leap from adjacent room and attack with all attacks plus a % to deal a massive hit which stuns the mob for one or two rounds, takes 60 moves and has 2 round lag time. Can hit wounded mobs. This would be useful for mobs that still hate the group. The tank could leap in, try to stun the mob so the group can follow and finish off the mob.

wa: tend wounds - 21 level warrior skill that heals wounds. heals anywhere from 5-20 hp, but has a lag time of 3 rounds and can only be done while resting. You must also have bandages in inventory, which would be bought at the store for a price of 1000 gold.
