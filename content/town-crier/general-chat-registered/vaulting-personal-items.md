<div class="post" data-post-id="38264" data-author="Teron" data-date="2017-05-31T08:32:22+00:00" data-subject="Re: Vaulting Personal Items">

1. Removing "personal" from quest items would make drachma unlimited to all your characters, which wasn't the intention of drachma. 

Though it could be considered, as to get one item, one has to 10-50 of quests by cycling for dozens of cps.

Also, if items are not personal, they'd be resold on auction.

I think most items need to be personal, but releasing the 20 least bought drachma items as forges or non-personal items shouldn't be too bad, imho.

2. There also could be quest heirloom items, which could be passed from one char to another, but they'd have to be tied to a person/account, which we don't have.

3. Could also be clan items, which would be a measure of how tough a clan is.

Just as I was going to type "I might be the first to think of the concept", I googled and found a few good rules for the clan armory:

[https://us.battle.net/forums/en/d3/topic/13816741604](https://us.battle.net/forums/en/d3/topic/13816741604)

Which is the only reasonable post on the topic, though.
