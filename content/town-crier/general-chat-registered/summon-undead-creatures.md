<div class="post" data-post-id="34875" data-author="Magi" data-date="2014-02-18T20:06:26+00:00" data-subject="Re: Summon Undead Creatures">

You get more duration to your summoned creatures as your Necro level increases past the minimum level to summon them.

For all the summons unlocked at or after level 40, duration is capped at the minimum time: 10 minutes.  Your Necro level never increases, so you're always the 'minimum level' to summon them.

It would be nice if it did continue, but I think the immortal consensus is that necromancers are pretty strong and don't need the help.
