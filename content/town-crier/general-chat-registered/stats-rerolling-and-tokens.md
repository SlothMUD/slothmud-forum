I think the tokens are fixed incor.  Here is the plan:

tri tokens will now work for everybody.  They will generally give you decent stats that you can play for now, and if you roll 10 or 20 of them you might get something you'd be willing to keep if you aren't too ambitious.

hex tokens are going to be just like they were.

oct tokens are going away.

Using some arbitary definitions, here are what I think the odds are for various categories of stats with hexes: terrible 1 in 3, barely playable 1 in 4, playable for now 1 in 3, good 1 in 10, very good 1 in 15, great 1 in 200

and with the new tris: terrible 1 in 25, barely playable 1 in 3, playable for now 1 in 2, good 1 in 7, very good 1 in 50, great 1 in 25000

So, if you're looking for what I described as "very good" or better, roll hexes; otherwise roll tris and you should get something decent pretty quick.  If you roll hexes and run out of hexes when you have garbage stats, roll a tri or two and you should be ok to play.
