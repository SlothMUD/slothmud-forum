The stargazer lily blooms at night in frosty mountain forests and 

fades before morning. Impossible to cultivate, the lily is occasionally

stumbled upon by fur traders. If picked before sunrise and placed in a 

dark container, the flower can persist for some time.

This is ingame and reasonably obtainable, but I still have some stuff to flesh out.
