<div class="post" data-post-id="37158" data-author="Teron" data-date="2016-07-31T06:15:32+00:00" data-subject="Re: Known Random Spellbook Mobs">

Mobs in the Amaryllian Pass and the Moors pop randomized spellbooks under 20 level, which include ones that are harder to get, than others, such as:

- darksight

- supplication

- dispel good

- goodberry

- summon undead

- animate dead

- banish undead

As far as I remember, all mobs pop the randomized sbs.

Noel

Pops some easy to get songbooks in his satchel. Sometimes doesn't pop the satchel, though, so keep killing him. 

Doesn't pop:

a faded songbook - sleep

a hallowed songbook - reprieve (irrc)

a braided book of mournful hymns - rina cruinne (elder priests of enea)

a parchment of sharp-notes - gods of war (easiest to get is Ei in the Crossroads)

In short, his list of bard spellbooks is fairly limited.

He spawns in a room with many exists and reappears on 3 different continents:

AH: the Judge's temple

SS: SS town, 3-4s from well

Island: Siren's Rock, 5w, all s from the East Bamboo Gate.
