Thanks Krom for enhancing Warrior a little, particularly for non-primes. Note for Warrior primes not too happy with their lot at the moment, this is apparently designed as a first set of adjustments, so to me that infers more is coming..

For those who missed it, here's the basics (correct me if I'm wrong):

<strong>For Warrior primes :</strong>

 \- frenzy duration increased a bit at higher levels (mine at 5x40 is now 90 seconds)

 \- frenzy now adds damage to non-melee attacks as well (backstab, deathgrip, deathtouch, wraithtouch, strike and circlestab) at \~50% of what its regular damage bonus is.

 \- berserk now adds damage to non-melee attacks, but at roughly 2x the rate of frenzy (still has 2.0ac penalty though). 

<strong>For Warrior non-primes :</strong>

 \- small boost to melee damage (physical attacks, not kick, backstab etc).

 \- frenzy duration increased a bit at higher levels.

 \- frenzy now adds damage to non-melee attacks as well (backstab, deathgrip, deathtouch, wraithtouch, strike and circlestab) at \~50% of what its regular damage bonus is.

 \- berserk now adds damage to non-melee attacks, but at roughly 2x the rate of frenzy (still has 2.0ac penalty though). 

 \- minimum hp bonuses at tertiary and quad levels. For tertiary you will never get less than 4 hp (not much different than now), for quad you will not receive less than 2 hp per level.

So there you have it. For primes, the main thing is the frenzy stab bonus imho. Substantial improvements for non-prime Warriors. Also may make Thief primes think again about excluding warrior from their classo, although yet to ascertain just how much an improvement it is.. stabbed a couple of mobs and little apparent difference, but too soon to tell really.
