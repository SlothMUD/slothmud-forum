Since no one is posting an opener recap, I will.

Mayor Zenobia alerted us to an enemy that surfaced on the cold continent of Thule. We were to find the sneaky spies, take the key fragments, forge the key and infiltrate the enemy lair to kill all the invaders.

Luckily, the spies have chosen to only come through the Ether Plains, so we found them quickly enough and reduced them to piles of dust, ashes and icicles - they were conveniently vulnerable to different schools of magic. We then found Sheikh Shabazz to forge us the key, so we could enter the enemy lair.

The enemy turned out to be a colony of ant-like creatures, ranging from relatively small, those who can kill a cow, to the gargantuan Queen Mother. We walked through the area, easily killing smaller ants, while exploring the caverns and looking for the queen.

The medium ants turned out to be splork mobs - for Reality - with some gold. While killing them, we popped:
