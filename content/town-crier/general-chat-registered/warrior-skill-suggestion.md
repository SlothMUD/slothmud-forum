Just a quick note, it's usually easiest to ask one of the healers for a heal or two before you (the 2.tank) die. I'd ask for one specific person to send a heal, if he doesn't respond ask a different person. Otherwise, either several people will send a heal and overheal, or everyone will assume someone else will heal then noone does. Also, if you wait until right after a round of combat before sending a grouptell, the person has a better chance of noticing and reacting. All that said, I try to pay attention to random hits and group hps, and toss a heal when necessary.

I'm not trying to imply that you need these tips RR, but they might be useful for newer players.
