Frobisher (William Lees) has just released a new Beta version of Wintin.Net.

The major addition for this realease is Wintin's chat facility...

Wintin's Chat facility allows you to establish private channels directly between your computer and those of other players. It is based on MudMaster Chat, which means you can communicate with MudMaster and Zmud users, as well as other Wintin users. Once chat channels are established, you can use them to send messages to other users or groups of users, to exchange files, and to 'snoop' another player's connection to their mud.
