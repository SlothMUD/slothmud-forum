I was thinking that since this is now a 8 class mud, it might make some sense to open up a pool of like 10/12 new skills/spells that everybody can pick only 2 from (this is purely conceptual in nature, so feel free to bash, flame, suggest, or actually implement). That way, everybody can actually semi-customize their character a bit more than what we already have. The issue Splork raised is that you'll have to come up with new equally balanced skills/spells. I'll propose some ideas here - bear in mind that you could only have 2 of those and they're permanent selections, can't be changed once chosen for your character although upon second look, it might be just 1 skill if the suggestions I have are that unbalanced:

Evade - Physical skill - adds 1 damage reduction point to your character.

Fade - Combat skill - combines disengage with invisibility spell.

Morph - Semi shapeshift skill - new form. Skills/spells to be determined.

Blackjack - Backstab skill using hammer-type weapons only.

Spiritual Armor - Spell shield for melee attacks.

Runic - Combat skill - chance to absorb mob's proc for 0 damage and gain some mana from it.

Spectral Mount - Special mount is summoned that can double as pet with very weak attack and has special bonus for charge skill. Pet is wraithformed.

Flashfire - Sets room onto fire that will damage mobs (room proc, must be outdoors)...

Acidweb - Combines web spell with weak version of poison.

Flashstroke - Combines blindness spell (like stun) with broadside skill

Mimic - Mimics the last action taken (can be very good or very bad), especially in groups.

Accuracy - Increases natural hitroll

Pummel - 2x strike, or improved critical hit... something...

Dance - set of 5 effects at random (almost like fortune, but costs mvs)

Lore - learn a limited subset of mob procs that you can then use later - honestly, who wouldn't want to be able to use certain mob procs themselves? You'd have to go get hit by the proc x amount of times before you completed learning it. - \*\*\* This could be made into a bard spell/skill instead of this... sounds like it could HELP them a lot!

Holy Fire - combination spell of curse/faerie fire/flamestrike damage in 1

Sinkhole - combination spell of disint damage with quicksand (like brambles)

That's just a start... any feedback or flames? <img src="/images/smilies/icon_e_smile.svg" alt=":)" title=":)" width="15" height="15" class="smiley nozoom">

My biggest fear, as a player is that I don't want to reach avatar and start being nerfed because they put something in early that tipped the balances of player power / time. It's always fantastic when we get something new in this game, we are blown away when something broken finally gets fixed after years and years... but people often quit when they get nerfed... And nerfs are the worst thing that can happen as it short changes players.

So in short, I'd rather not have something, than to have it and see it nerfed... and things are still kind of sorting themselves out at the moment. There is still a settling going on.
