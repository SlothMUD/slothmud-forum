<div class="post" data-post-id="25675" data-author="Rynquald" data-date="2008-08-07T22:43:38+00:00">

It happens once in a while.

It's hard to find the owner of a char's worth of eq if it actually belonged to a new player, since none of the eq is likely to be particularly non-standard. If it's from an ultra new player, it's also possible that they gave up playing altogether after corpsing.

As for the first set of eq, if you've got a full set of eq from a higher level char, there will almost certainly be something personal that you can id them from. If you can find out who the eq you have belongs to, but they don't log enough for you to catch them, checking last player to see if they belong to a clan would be a good way to find someone that can contact them. Or at least someone willing to hold the eq until the owner comes back.

Personally, the couple of times I've been carting around eq with an indeterminable owner, I've posted and waited a couple of weeks before giving it to a newb or something.
