The forge note:

The latest in Thalassan fashion is here!  Raven-black is the

new fuschia this season as thrifty tailors redesign old garments

into stunning new eveningwear!

Light shimmerweave culled from a voluminous garment is reworked

into a more fashionable design using the experience imbued into a

needle inherited for generations.  Clairvoyant eyewear is a must,

allowing seamstresses to preempt any potential mistakes in this

delicate redesign.  Insubstantial ribbons can be added to complete

a ravishing minerva, or ruffles from a stylish cravat can be used

to tailor a handsome tailcoat.

Then, using a simple dye easily brewed by local alchemists, this

fashion masterpiece is properly colored and enhanced.  A wereraven

feather is dissolved in the acidic oils extracted from the petals

of a ravenous black flower to create the trademark coracinus color.

Delicate skin from young blue dragonwhelps is added to the dye to

provide additional protection as well as a stylish tinge of blue.

Fashion-minded individuals may send us the necessary materials,

as well as a nominal fee, and our master tailors and seamstresses

will immediately manufacture this wondrous eveningwear for you!

Those without direct access to us can seek out well connected

and well dressed gentlemen, and they will be sure to help place

your order.
