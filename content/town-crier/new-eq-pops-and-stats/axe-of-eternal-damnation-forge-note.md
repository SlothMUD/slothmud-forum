Axe of Eternal Damnation has no cps and no forge note listed. Think we popped it off of some silly mob in Kendermore... either way, I tried adding the note text to forge and it wouldn't let me, so I'm adding it here:

> <strong>a parchment being consumed by black flames wrote:</strong>
> It has been told through legend and fable that once, eons ago, a demon lord
> 
> walked the land with a mighty axe that could call forth the spawns of
> 
> hell. The demon lord struck terror into the hearts of the innocent
> 
> throughout the world until a legendary immortal drove the demon lord into
> 
> oblivion and freed the world from misery. To prevent the rebirth of this
> 
> great evil, the immortal destroyed the axe. I have found evidence in my
> 
> research to lead me to believe that this great axe can be reconstructed.
> 
> However, the components required to forge this mighty weapon are rare and
> 
> strewn across the world. The primary ingredient, the template for this
> 
> evil weapon, was left in the guard of the dwarven nation in the form of a
> 
> powerful broad axe. The blade of the axe requires a huge gem that is hard
> 
> enough to pierce the skin of the gods. Somewhere in the land there is an
> 
> ancient tablet fashioned from onyx that can be melted down and used to coat
> 
> the weapon with pure onyx. The entire weapon must be wrapped in the thick
> 
> hide of a great beholder-kin in order to harness the magic needed to
> 
> enchant the axe. Finally, a sturdy metal file is needed to sharpen the
> 
> gemstone-covered edge of the blade. Bring me these, and I will attempt to
> 
> reconstruct the legendary weapon. ------ Preva
