Just started a new character with Druid prime again, the idea of shapeshifting really appeals to me. A couple of questions related to this:

1\) To be able to shapeshift requires that you travel very light, you can carry almost nothing. I was wondering, do the rest of you even bother to use a backpack, or is there a trick to getting more carrying capacity with a druid.

2\) When I do have my possessions down to the level needed to shapeshift, I run into another problem with looting the corpse; it gives me the error that I’m out of room when trying to get the coins. Is this just a problem with the lower level animals like the Cat and Ferret, because they don’t have enough strength? And if so, at what level will it get to be easy to use the animal form for all my fighting, Drake maybe?

3\) By the way, can the Frog actually fight at its level? Seems like a silly animal for level 10, maybe they should have made it a salamander…

<div class="post" data-post-id="6035" data-author="Avatar" data-date="2005-05-07T11:57:01+00:00" data-subject="">

Druid primes start with a green cape, which is a sort of backpack. You can put your stuff in there and wear it, then you won't have stuff in your inventory and it should be easier to pick stuff up. It does get easier as you level, because your carrying capacity will increase and the strength of the shift forms increases.

The frog is a pretty good fighter, once you have frog you'll level pretty easily for a few levels.

I assume that you're using potions while you're low level. It's quite handy to get those armor potions (red?) and strength potions (orange).
