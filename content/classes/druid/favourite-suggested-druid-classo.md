I voted for dr/cl, even though I play a dr/ne.  I've been a little unsatisfied with dr/ne.  Necro is a great combination for druid, but with the way followers are structured now it doesn't seem best to have necro second.  A druid with necro second gets the same followers as a druid with necro quad, once he gets the levels up there.  Cleric on the other hand will get better supplication forms if the cleric class is second rather than third or fourth.  So my recommendation would be dr/xx/xx/ne for players who intend to reach avatar some day.

The druid prime regen bonus seems like an optimal way to compensate for the lesser mana regen of a caster/noncaster class.  A dr/nc should regen better (outdoors) than a non-druid ca/ca.  I haven't though of any combination that would benefit from that, really.  Perhaps a dr/th could stab a mob then use battleshift to shift and finish the fight, similarly dr/mo might grip then battleshift, but I don't know how effective that would be.
