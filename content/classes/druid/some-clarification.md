<div class="post" data-post-id="3020" data-author="Alberich" data-date="2004-11-24T10:46:07+00:00" data-subject="">

the major, all-around upside to dr primes are their insane regen - even nerfed, it is not hard for a lowbie druid (3x40-ish) to regen 100+ mana outdoors. aside from that, I do believe dr primes get bonuses to their shifted forms, as well as less lag when battleshifting.

as for your questions about classo... it really depends on how you want to play. druids are all about insane regen, whereas warriors are about high hps/good ac - if you want to sit in groups, a dr/wa would be a better choice than a wa/dr (and a dr/cl or dr/ma would be better than either). I could not think of any real reason to make a wa/dr, except possibly for that regen bonus - but really, if you can shift, you don't need a warriors hps, and if you are a warrior prime, you have no real reason to ever shift (yes, I know people go on about shifting for crs, but it's just as easy to simply log on another char once you corpse, and the lower dr forms cannot kill anything that might have looted, so I've never been too impresed by that).
