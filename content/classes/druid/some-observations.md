<div class="post" data-post-id="1363" data-author="Alberich" data-date="2004-08-12T10:28:37+00:00" data-subject="">

different styles of play for different classes... 

druids are not a blasting class, never where, probably never will be - that is why we have mages.

if you think the shifted forms are not good, you either do not have very many levels, or have made no effort whatsoever to learn to use them.

I have seen a small (2x40ish) druid solo mobs I would not even attempt to kill at lvl 15 avatar.

druids, from what I have seen (and I do not own a primary druid, and my one char with druid in class is still on valk, so not claiming to be any great expert) is a class that is all about smarts - using your head, and finding creative ways to do truly unholy things to mobs, using all the skills/forms at your disposal. You have battle shift, rapid shift, equanimity, truly ungodly regen, and a number of other huge benefits - if you try to play like a traditional caster class, you are not going to be able to benefit from most of that.

Personally, I think that, properly played, druids are one of (if not the) most powerful classes in the game, right now - it just requires a bit more effort and thought than playing, say, a mage <img src="/images/smilies/icon_e_smile.svg" alt=":)" title=":)" width="15" height="15" class="smiley nozoom">

<div class="post" data-post-id="1366" data-author="Ender" data-date="2004-08-12T12:47:33+00:00" data-subject="Classes....">

Few notes:

1\) Size is very subjective in this game. One person is talking about a 2x40 druid as small, the other is trying to get to 1x40.

2\) I'll say the same thing as I did for necro... 'like'-spells should NOT be identical to the original. If other classes have better versions of the same spells, it's probably because you're stronger than they are in other areas.

3\) Magic room and blasting may be better... live with it. Druid is a subclass of CLERIC, not mage. If you wanted to blast, mage is your obvious choice here.

4\) I think mage is a tough class to keep out of the classo, simply because they seem to rather powerful... Even quad casters are incredibly powerful, walking around with far more AC than I would consider justifiable.

5\) Every classo has a different play-style. Every player has a different play-style. Some people like a challenge, some can't hack it. There are shadow characters... consider how difficult they are to play. You only make about 1/2 as much xp in a group as you would normally, and a vast number of abilities are unavailable. I've even seen a rare few that are leveling a SINGLE CLASS. These are self imposed challenges above those inflicted by the game. If you don't want a challenge, or if you just want to play what you know... stick with what you know. Don't complain that a mage is a better mage than your druid, or that a cleric is a better cleric than your druid. Play a mage/cleric.

6\) I play a thief/warrior. I like to explore and I die a lot. This suits me.
