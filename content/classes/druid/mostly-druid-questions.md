<div class="post" data-post-id="22058" data-author="Rynquald" data-date="2008-01-18T01:05:48+00:00" data-subject="">

Save eq makes you more likely to take half damage from spells, which sounds nice until you realize that even if you wear no saves eq, you're almost certainly saving most of the time vs. mob-cast spells.

AC is exclusively for making mobs miss, once a mob makes a successful attack roll against your ac, it doesn't matter what ac you have as to how much damage you take. Spells like Sanc, Aegis, Fluidity and the damage reduction skill are what literally reduces the damage you take form a hit.

One of the most common complaints about shifting that I've heard is that it costs far too much mana to be useful for xp runs, which is entirely corrent. Imo shifting isn't designed for xp, or any kind of sustained killing, it's for bringing down something you couldn't kill otherwise, but in a highly inefficient fasion.

You're right about the regen though, no matter what you kill shifted, regenning will normally be by far the biggest part of the run (time-wise, and depending on how much regen you actually have of course).

If you're after Lyme xp as a caster, you're probably best off running stab/blast mobs, but without the stab part <img src="/images/smilies/icon_e_smile.svg" alt=":)" title=":)" width="15" height="15" class="smiley nozoom">

<div class="post" data-post-id="26144" data-author="jezer" data-date="2008-09-04T15:48:56+00:00" data-subject="">

I've tried to work out which is mathematically better for the druid avatar light.

\+1 dam +4 mana regen or +2 dam

I worked out for me at the moment it costs...

5 mana per tick to maintain tarantula form (25dam without light)

19 mana per tick to maintain phoenix form (24dam without light)

<strong>If I were to choose the +1 dam +4mr light....</strong>

I would be able to run 26% longer as phoenix and 500% longer as tarantula. I would also do 4% damage more as tarantula and 4.1% more damage as phoenix as long as I ran.

<strong>If I were to choose the +2 dam light....</strong>

I would do 8.3% more damage as Phoenix and 8% more damage as tarantula.

Using this information two things becomes clear...

The shorter the fight, the better it is to have the +2dam light.

The more mana it costs to maintain your form, the more the +2 damage light will work better.

If it were to cost 55 mana per tick to main piasa form, the +4mr light only adds 7.8% to your running time, which quickly starts to near the percentage increase that you get through the extra dam point on the +2dam light.

So the question is really impossible to answer without first knowing how many rounds the fight will last... 

But you can follow this rule of thumb, <strong>if you are going to be running large mobs where you only can survive a few rounds a +2 dam light will be slightly more advantageous</strong>

However when you consider the variety of mobs on which one runs, and variable length of the battles, the true difference between the two types of lights is not much... and overall I'd say that the +4mr +dam light probably works out better overall.
