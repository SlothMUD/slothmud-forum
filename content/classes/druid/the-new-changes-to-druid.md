Judge has been working hard on fixing some of the druid problems it seems. I have both good and bad news to report... Please update this thread as you find stuff...

Mythic forms at least have more hitpoints.

Mythic forms have more damage... +4 on my phoenix.

You can prac when your shifted.

I lost 3 mana regen points standing in the changes. Judge said there was a bug in the old save command that cause points to stack.

I got aggroed as a ferret killing the kalamari. I think this is a bug and it's being looked at. Another 90m xp lost on bugs.

The biggest change however, is that returning back to human form when I was shifted phoenix, does not return me near full human health. This has always been the case since I made tarantula form and it's going to be hard for me to get used too.

When I report shifted, it does not report the hp's of my human form so I am guessing that eudaemons will now heal shifted players.

I'm told a lot of bugs quaffing potions while shifted etc are also fixed.

So all in all, it's a mixed bag for players so far. A lot of bugs have been fixed that were annoying, but also the ones that made it easier for us.

The new hp's and dam is awesome, but the having to heal after unshifting is definately going to cut the solo xp run.
