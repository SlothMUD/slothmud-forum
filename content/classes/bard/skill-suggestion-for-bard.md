I fail to see how poking things with sharp objects falls under the realm of 'bardish abilities', or what possible use a singlestab could have anyways.  Most people with bard in classo if they plan on soloing would have monk or thief anyways.

As to the ac thing - thieves roll dex first too of course, and the bards would have the same crap ac as a thief, due to having low str without wa sec.  Not to mention that +dam eq has crap ac for the most part, so you would have a bard with a single stab with a low modifier, and almost no damage because bards tend to wear +charisma eq (or I suppose, a bard with reasonable damage and no charsisma gear, which kinda defeats the purpose of a bard in the first place).

I don't see how it would hurt or be overpowered, but it seems kinda silly.
