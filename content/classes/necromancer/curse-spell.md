<div class="post" data-post-id="6609" data-author="Alberich" data-date="2005-05-27T15:11:29+00:00" data-subject="">

curse/damn are support spells - they basically lower the mobs hitroll, and stack with blind. They are not really 'offensive' any more than blind or web are, as they don't damage the mob or anything.

<div class="post" data-post-id="6611" data-author="12345" data-date="2005-05-27T15:35:03+00:00" data-subject="">

It's actually a pretty cool spell. As Alberich pointed out, it lowers the mob's hitroll. The effect would be much the same as lowering your AC a point. Mostly useful for tankish types to reduce the damage taken during battle and against group chop mobs, such as Ersa on Valk. Can make a difficult battle much, much easier or be completely worthless depending on the mob.
