Why Don’t Followers Assist?

Jorgen normally runs around with 3 pets in tow; a lares, a spectre, and a ghoul. When cruising the streets of Newbatia, things seem to work fine most of the time. He typically attacks thugs by blinding, striking, then ordering followers to assist him (Order Followers Assist Jorgen). After the first round of combat normally all 3 join in. Occasionally 2, or maybe even 1, will join in after the first round and he’ll have to issue the order again. Very infrequently he may have to issue the order 3 times.

This is not what I’ve been experiencing in the gang’s hideout, especially on the trail leading there. The majority of the time I’m seeing only 1 pet join in, no matter how many times I issue the order. I’ve even tried issuing the order for each missing pet by name (Order Ghoul Assist Jorgen). This last time the spectre was the only one to help for most battles; I don’t recall if it was the same pet on the previous times I’ve tried this area.

1) Do your pets decide to follow orders based on the area?

2) Does lighting or the mob hiding have anything to do with it? I normally have both Darksight and Detect Invisibility on, so maybe I’m seeing the hiding thugs that 2 of the pets can’t see, although you would think they’d spot them when I’m punching them.

3) Is there anything I can do to improve my pets response to orders?

Thanks.

The master in general is a pain.  He's inpressively more powerful than anything else in the area, and the room he's in makes him difficult to fight.

He summons help, and he's in a room that makes tells, grouptells, etc. impossible.  It also make using scrolls impossible.  Didn't try ordering followers, but it wouldn't surprize me if that's impossible too.

Casted several cures while in there, half didn't work, some were weaked... one got boosted to a greater heal.  Offensive spells were equally wacky.  All in all, not a good place to cast.

Personal recommendation, bring a lot of really good potions with you.
