<div class="post" data-post-id="23458" data-author="Rynquald" data-date="2008-03-26T23:46:07+00:00">

It's been a while, but I don't think spiritualist hits hard enough to be infeasible to solo. If you have a char with ac, you can pretty much just lead with damn and try to land a quick acid blind (if you have mage), then self-heal and pray for chops. A nightmare will help of course.

If you're looking to solo with a low-ac caster and have a healthy disregard for your safety you can just take the hits and spam heals at yourself, but that's pretty inefficient.

Macus are almost always the best specs to use, although if you want to gut you'll need an actual specularem. If you do want to gut it would still be best to wield a macu for most of the fight and just switch at ph though.

And the chances of chopping yourself are slim, I wouldn't worry about that danger compared to the danger of the mob.
