It takes about 3 people to successfully drag down the ancient stag.  Tank with -7 or -8 helps tremendously, but you'll still take quite a beating, so have sanc and someone dedicated to keeping the tank alive.  Lead with curse and web, then have two people fireball the hell out of him.  Blind is nice if you get lucky, but will generally cause more pain than just nuking him.  If you have three 2x20s that can meet these requirements, it should be doable.  Just stay organized and have your recalls handy.
