While it is true that you can get a 10 10 10 10 10 on an oct, I think you miss the point. When I have kept track 2 out of 3 rolls have a low stat less than 10, which would be rerolled by an Oct. While you may still not end up with a great stat, it is effectively like rolling 2-3 other tokens, particularly when you roll a lot of them together. What it ultimatly boils down to is if you roll 20 octs in a row, statictically you would need to roll 60 other tokens to get the same results (the risk being you roll a triple 18 with a stat of 9 that it rerolls).

In the end I agree rolling 1 or 2 octs and expecting a good result is naive, but rolling 20 or 25 in a row really is like rolling 60 to 75 triangles.
